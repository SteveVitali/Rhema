# ca-sb243-companion-chatbots-2025-text
- **URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB243 · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 (agenda 3)
- **What it establishes:** SB 243 (Padilla), Ch. 677, Stats. 2025 (signed October 2025; operative Jan. 1, 2026; reporting from July 1, 2027). Defines "companion chatbot" as "an artificial intelligence system with a natural language interface that provides adaptive, human-like responses to user inputs and is capable of meeting a user's social needs" (with exemptions). Requires a notification that the chatbot "is artificially generated and not human" where a reasonable person would be misled; a protocol "for preventing the production of suicidal ideation, suicide, or self-harm content" with crisis referrals, published on the website; for a user the operator "knows is a minor": AI disclosure, break reminders "at least every three hours," and measures against sexually explicit material; annual reports to the Office of Suicide Prevention (no identifiers); private right of action ($1,000 per violation). The bill does not require operators to verify or determine user age and contains no identity, logging, or retention mandate.
- **Key passages:** as above.
- **Reliability:** Primary (chaptered bill text).
- **Relevance to this project:** Agenda 3. Layer 3 obligation of classification (self-harm detection) without retention or identity; the "knows is a minor" trigger is the hook that SB 1119 turns into an age-assurance duty.
