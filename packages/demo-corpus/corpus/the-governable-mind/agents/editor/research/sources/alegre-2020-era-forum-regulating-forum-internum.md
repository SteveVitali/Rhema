# alegre-2020-era-forum-regulating-forum-internum
- **URL:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12027-020-00633-7 (reached via Springer cookie-fallback) · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 legal/philosophical
- **What it establishes:** Susie Alegre, "Regulating around freedom in the 'forum internum'," ERA Forum (2020). The right is absolute ("there can never be a justification for interfering with the right to freedom of thought"); states must legislate against private as well as public interference; data reveals "how we think"; the focus is on private-sector inference and manipulation, with no developed treatment of limits on the state's own surveillance architecture.
- **Key passages:** "laws must be in place, not only to prevent state actions that could interfere with our rights to freedom in the 'forum internum,' but also prohibiting others from doing so." · "prohibiting the kind of practices and techniques which threaten to undermine the right, no matter who is using them." · "the granular nature of the data available on us does not only reveal who we are and where we live, it reveals how we think."
- **Reliability:** Primary; the author is an advocate for the strong reading.
- **Relevance to this project:** Agenda 10 (l.68). Supports "argues from its absoluteness to a duty to regulate the private platforms," with the nuance that she names state action first ("not only to prevent state actions"); the draft's "inversion" should be softened to "chiefly."
