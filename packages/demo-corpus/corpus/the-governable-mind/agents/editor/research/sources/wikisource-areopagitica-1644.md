# wikisource-areopagitica-1644
- **URL:** https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Areopagitica_(1644) · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 1
- **What it establishes:** 1644-spelling text; the three quotations, "mischievous and libellous"/"the fire and the executioner," and Milton's approval of the prior registration Order, all verbatim; header dates publication Nov 23, 1644. Does not name the Stationers (only "old patentees and monopolizers in the trade of book-selling").
- **Key passages:** "advising ye better then your selves have done in that Order publisht next before this, that no book be Printed, unlesse the Printers and the Authors name, or at least the Printers be register'd."
- **Reliability:** Primary text (djvu transcription of the 1644 pamphlet).
- **Relevance to this project:** C24; §I, §VI.
