# hr9917-ai-kill-switch-act-text-2026
- **URL:** https://www.congress.gov/119/bills/hr9917/BILLS-119hr9917ih.pdf · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 policy
- **What it establishes:** H.R. 9917, AI Kill Switch Act, introduced July 23, 2026 (Lieu, Moran), 15 pp., full text extracted. Adds HSA §2220F. Covered entity: operates a covered technology, makes it available 'through a programmatic interface, hosted service, or other similar mechanism,' ≥$500M revenue from it; exemption for personal/academic/non-commercial use. Covered technology: >$100M compute. Definitions updated annually by rule with 'the manner in which the model weights ... are made available' as a factor. Mandated capabilities: stop inference; terminate user access; suspend access re 'an account, user, or use pattern identified by such covered entity or the Secretary as posing a risk of' a covered incident OR 'a violation of law or the terms of service'; shut down. DHS order on a covered incident; preserve weights and telemetry; audit/telemetry/on-site inspection. Reconsideration within 48h (no stay; silence = denial in 5 days); D.C. Circuit petition within 60 days, no stay, no timetable. FOIA exemption. No sunset; no user-content logging mandate.
- **Key passages:** '(iii) Suspend access to such technology with respect to an account, user, or use pattern identified by such covered entity or the Secretary as posing a risk of any of the following: (I) A covered incident. (II) A violation of law or the terms of service of such technology.' · '(C) The capabilities of covered technology, the deployment of such technology, and the manner in which the model weights of such technology are made available.' · 'such petition does not stay such order' · 'may request review of such order in the United States Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit on petition filed not later than 60 days after such order.'
- **Reliability:** Primary; enrolled bill text (GPO).
- **Relevance to this project:** Q2; l.144 and l.260: 'reviewable in court within days' is wrong; the ToS trigger is omitted; annual scope reset bears on Q7.
