# zwillgen-state-chatbot-laws-jun-2026
- **URL:** https://www.zwillgen.com/publication/chatbot-laws-coming-to-a-state-near-you/ · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 2
- **What it establishes:** As of June 2026, 12 states have chatbot laws (most effective Jan. 1, 2027): self-harm detection protocols and crisis referral universal; six states require annual reporting of referral counts; Colorado age estimation for all users; New York age confirmation for companion features; no logging/retention or identity-verification mandates.
- **Key passages:** "Each law requires the operator to maintain a protocol for detecting expressions of suicidal ideation or self-harm"
- **Reliability:** Secondary (law-firm survey); industry orientation.
- **Relevance to this project:** Report §6; draft l.20 (C15), l.254.
