- **What it establishes:** Covington summary of Ofcom's open letter of Nov. 8, 2024: chatbots enabling users to share generated content with other users are "user-to-user services"; services whose generative tools can produce pornographic material must implement "highly effective age assurance" by Jan. 17, 2025; no identity, logging, or retention duty; standalone chatbots not addressed.
