# eu-gpai-code-of-practice-measure-3-5
- **URL:** https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/code-of-practice-overview/ · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 policy
- **What it establishes:** EU GPAI Code of Practice overview: Measure 3.5 requires independent external evaluators pre- and post-market unless the model is 'similarly safe or safer' or none found after a 20-business-day public call; evaluator qualifications (PhD or peer-reviewed work; 3 years); 'sufficient numbers of independent external evaluators'; access incl. minimal-safeguard variants; non-retaliation.
- **Key passages:** 'sufficient numbers of independent external evaluators' · qualifications: 'a relevant PhD or peer-reviewed work, experience in developing model evaluation methods, [and] at least 3 years of relevant work or research experience.'
- **Reliability:** Secondary summary of a primary code; annotated mirror.
- **Relevance to this project:** Q1(e): plurality and qualification without accreditation; no sunset; the nearest existing regime to the draft's third condition.
