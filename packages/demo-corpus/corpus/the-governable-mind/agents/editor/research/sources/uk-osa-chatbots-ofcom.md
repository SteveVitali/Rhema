# uk-osa-chatbots-ofcom
- **URL:** https://www.manchesterdigital.com/post/pinsentmasons/online-safety-act-duties-cover-gen-ai-and-chatbots-ofcom-confirms ; https://www.onlinesafetynetwork.uk/analysis/chatbots-and-the-online-safety-act/ (Ofcom's own explainer at ofcom.org.uk was behind a verification wall) · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 1
- **What it establishes:** Chatbots fall under the OSA when user-to-user, search, or pornographic-content services; "highly effective age assurance" required only to keep children from the most harmful content "where this is allowed on the platform"; no identity-verification or chat-log retention duty (C15).
- **Key passages:** "services allowing users to share text, images, or videos generated by a chatbot with other users will be classified as user-to-user services." / "using highly effective age assurance to prevent children from encountering the most harmful types of content where this is allowed on the platform." / "the coverage appears incomplete."
- **Reliability:** Secondary (law-firm summary of Ofcom's Nov. 2024 letter; independent legal analysis, 7 July 2025).
- **Relevance to this project:** C15.
