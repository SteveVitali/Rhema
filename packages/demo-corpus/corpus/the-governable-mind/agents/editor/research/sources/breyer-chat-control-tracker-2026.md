# breyer-chat-control-tracker-2026
- **URL:** https://www.patrick-breyer.de/en/posts/chat-control/ · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 (agenda 3)
- **What it establishes:** Former MEP Patrick Breyer's running tracker of the EU CSA Regulation ("Chat Control 2.0"). Council negotiating mandate adopted 13 November 2025 after "mandatory chat control was finally removed"; end-to-end encrypted services out of scope of detection; but the mandate retains "voluntary" provider scanning "at the provider's discretion" and age verification / under-16 app restrictions. Parliament's mandate (14 November 2023): hash-matching of known material only, targeted at suspects, "no mandatory age verification." Trilogues: 9 Dec. 2025, 26 Feb., 16 Apr., 11 May, 29 June 2026, without agreement. Interim regulation ("Chat Control 1.0," voluntary scanning): Parliament rejected a second extension in March 2026 and it "expired on 4 April 2026." Breyer's warning: "mandatory age verification resulting in the end of anonymous communication."
- **Key passages:** "mandatory chat control was finally removed"; "no mandatory age verification"; "expired on 4 April 2026."
- **Reliability:** Secondary; advocacy (Pirate Party) but the most detailed public timeline; dates cross-checked against The Register (July 9, 2026).
- **Relevance to this project:** Agenda 3. Client-side scanning is not in the Council text; the live identity mechanism in the EU file is age verification, contested between Council and Parliament; the mandatory regime is not enacted as of Sept. 2026.
