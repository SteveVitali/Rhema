# flexheg-part-i-2025-scope-and-abuse
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.15093 · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 policy
- **What it establishes:** Petrie, Aarne, Ammann, Dalrymple, flexHEG Part I (2025), full PDF via text proxy (supplements petrie-flexheg-2025). Governs only the compute-intensive frontier; consumer GPUs appear only as a stockpiling/circumvention route; Appendix A concedes the original absolute-privacy vision fails (some information must be shared; physical inspection needs a registry of owners and locations); acknowledges 'significant potential for abuse' with multi-state rule approval and verification-only configurations as mitigations.
- **Key passages:** 'FlexHEGs will likely only be able to effectively govern the most compute-intensive frontier of AI development' (p. 16) · 'Occasionally rogue actors might attempt to stockpile non-flexHEG AI chips or consumer GPUs' (p. 13) · 'Physical inspections in turn require a registry of device owners and locations, further undermining original vision of absolute privacy' (App. A) · 'a comprehensive, global regime aimed at controlling the use of compute would have significant potential for abuse' (pp. 14–15).
- **Reliability:** Primary; ARIA-commissioned; safety-aligned.
- **Relevance to this project:** Q4: l.286 holds for Part I; the privacy retreat and abuse admission bear on §XI and §XIV.
