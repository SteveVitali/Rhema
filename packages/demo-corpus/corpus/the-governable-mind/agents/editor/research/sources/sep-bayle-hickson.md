# sep-bayle-hickson
- **URL:** https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/bayle/ · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 1
- **What it establishes:** Bayle's erring-conscience doctrine in the *Philosophical Commentary* (1686–88); the sincere-persecutor objection is the "Persecutor Paradox" (Solère 2016) and "the debate remains open" — so no single "standard reading" of Bayle's reply. Supports C18; qualifies C19.
- **Key passages:** "if the truth is unknown to us, it loses all its rights over us; and that, if error appears to us in the form of the truth, then it acquires all rights over us." / "The debate remains open."
- **Reliability:** Secondary, scholarly (SEP, Hickson, rev. Jan 12 2023); quotes Bayle directly.
- **Relevance to this project:** C18, C19; §I.
