# deazley-commentary-licensing-act-1662
- **URL:** https://www.copyrighthistory.org/cam/tools/request/showRecord.php?id=commentary_uk_1662 · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 1
- **What it establishes:** Settles the Licensing Act intervals: in force 1662–1679 and 1685–1695; renewed 1664, 1665; lapsed 1679; renewed 1685; expired May 1695; Commons rejected renewal 11 Feb 1695.
- **Key passages:** 'In force between 1662 and 1679, and then again between 1685 and 1695' / 'until May 1695 when … it finally expired and pre-publication censorship of the English press ceased.'
- **Reliability:** Secondary; scholarly commentary.
- **Relevance to this project:** C46, C78
