- **What it establishes:** Page pinpoints in Alegre, [2017] E.H.R.L.R. Issue 3, 221–233 (running heads read from the PDF). The "offering to the outside world" sentence is on p. 226; the absoluteness → duty-to-regulate-the-private-sector inference is stated at p. 225 and p. 222.
- **Key passages:** p. 226: "as technology develops more complex ways to surveille us and analyse the resulting data, it becomes less clear which thoughts we are offering to the outside world and which are being extracted without our knowledge or understanding." · p. 225: "Given the absolute nature of the right to freedom of thought, once an interference or limitation is established, it will be unlawful and cannot be justified. … they have a duty to protect us from others who seek to interfere … This should include an obligation to establish appropriate legal and regulatory frameworks to prevent the private sector from engaging in practices that interfere" · p. 222: "new legal and policy responses are required as a matter of urgency to regulate and limit business practices or state interventions that risk interference with freedom of thought."
