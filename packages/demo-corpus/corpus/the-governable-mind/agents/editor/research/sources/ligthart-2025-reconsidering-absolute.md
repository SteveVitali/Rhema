# ligthart-2025-reconsidering-absolute
- **URL:** https://academic.oup.com/hrlr/article/25/3/ngaf018/8155294 · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 2
- **What it establishes:** Ligthart, HRLR 25:3 (2025): categorical absoluteness unpersuasive; proposes implied limitations (therapeutic alteration; imminent harm; emergency disclosure) with an absolute essence (no punishment for thoughts; no forced disclosure of political/moral views; no bypassing of autonomy); 'beyond reach' premise 'evidently false'.
- **Key passages:** "Categorical insistence on the absolute nature ... is unpersuasive" · the premise that inner thoughts are beyond the reach of state officials is "evidently false"
- **Reliability:** Primary scholarly source; independent.
- **Relevance to this project:** Report §1.3, §4′(A); draft l.74, l.254.
