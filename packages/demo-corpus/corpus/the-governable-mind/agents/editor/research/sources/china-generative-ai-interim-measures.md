# china-generative-ai-interim-measures
- **URL:** https://www.chinalawtranslate.com/en/generative-ai-interim/ (via a reader proxy) ; https://fpf.org/blog/unveiling-chinas-generative-ai-regulation/ (on the April 2023 draft) · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 1
- **What it establishes:** Non-liberal contrast for C15. Final Interim Measures (eff. 15 Aug. 2023): no explicit real-name article (the draft's Art. 9 had one); Art. 11 confidentiality of "users' usage records"; Art. 14 providers must "store the relevant records and report to the relevant departments." Real-name obligations flow from the Cybersecurity Law generally.
- **Key passages:** Art. 14: "store the relevant records and report to the relevant departments in charge." / FPF on draft Art. 9: "require users to provide real identity information in accordance with the Cybersecurity Law."
- **Reliability:** Primary translation (China Law Translate) + secondary (FPF, June 2023, draft only).
- **Relevance to this project:** C15; §VIII (other checker).
