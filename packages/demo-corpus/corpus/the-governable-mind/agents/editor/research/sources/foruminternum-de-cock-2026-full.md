# foruminternum-de-cock-2026-full
- **URL:** https://foruminternum.org/ ; https://foruminternum.org/brief ; https://foruminternum.org/resources ; working paper DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21633712 (also SSRN 7194178) · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 legal/philosophical (agenda 4, 10)
- **What it establishes:** Caroline De Cock's project. Working paper: "Protecting the Thinking Process in Human-AI Interaction: The Forum Interstitiale Between Thought and Expression" (July 2026), which "proposes a third, the forum interstitiale, and sets out what building it would require. It tests every analogy existing law offers, from search queries and evidentiary privilege to protected reading, aggregated records, European correspondence and data protection law, private diaries, and neural data." Also: Tech Policy Press article (Jan. 2025) on AI Act output filters and freedom of thought; book "AI Tools, Not Gods" (BTF Press, 2026). The site's structure: forum internum (absolute, "the room inside your head"); forum externum (limitable); forum interstitiale, "the anteroom where a thought gets dressed before it goes out into the world, and can be sent back in if its owner changes her mind" (search queries, drafts, exploratory prompts, confessions typed to machines). Standard: "Give that space a name and a standard of its own, heightened, not absolute, and the twenty million logs, the opposite rulings handed down in the same week, and the opaque filters all have to answer to something they do not answer to today." Derivation is explicitly one-directional and non-absolute: "None of this reaches the room inside your head, and under this proposal nothing ever will. The forum interstitiale only works upwards. It lifts the anteroom out of the unprotected class it currently falls into." Concrete rules deferred: "Stating the line is the easy part. Drawing it inside specific instruments is the work." The brief defines "thought manipulation" (what a system shapes in what you are given) and "thought moderation" (what a system decides you are not given). Site cites the 20M-log order (Bloomberg Law), Warner v. Gilbarco (Justia), Heppner (hrlegalist PDF), Chatrie, Altman's privilege call, and a German court's Google AI Overviews liability case (LoC, July 17, 2026).
- **Key passages:** "heightened, not absolute"; "only works upwards"; "the anteroom where a thought gets dressed before it goes out into the world"; "Stating the line is the easy part."
- **Reliability:** Primary statement of the author's position; advocacy-research site by an independent scholar/consultant; working paper is a preprint.
- **Relevance to this project:** l.76 (De Cock characterization; "descends from the interior" vs. "standing beside"). Her own text supports the draft's characterization: the anteroom is a "third room," its standard is deliberately below absolute, and its protection is built upward from the unprotected class, not downward from Article 18's core. She names the same object (drafts, prompts, search queries) and the same evidence (the logs, Heppner, Chatrie).
