# eu-ai-act-article-12-logging
- **URL:** https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/12/ · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 2
- **What it establishes:** Art. 12 AI Act: automatic logging over the lifetime of high-risk systems only; general-purpose chatbots fall under Chapter V.
- **Key passages:** "High-risk AI systems shall technically allow for the automatic recording of events (logs) over the lifetime of the system."
- **Reliability:** Primary text via an independent explainer site.
- **Relevance to this project:** Report §6; draft l.20 (C15).
