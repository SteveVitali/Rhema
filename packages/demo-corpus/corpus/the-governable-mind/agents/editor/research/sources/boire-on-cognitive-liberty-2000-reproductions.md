# boire-on-cognitive-liberty-2000-reproductions
- **URL:** https://www.cognitiveliberty.org/on-cognitive-liberty-boire/ (also https://cognitive-liberty.online/on-cognitive-liberty/ and the MAPS PDF of Part I, https://bibliography.maps.org/resources/download/12520; all read via reader proxy; the original JCL page http://www.cognitiveliberty.org/ccle1/1jcl/1jcl7.htm is behind a bot challenge) · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 1 slice B
- **What it establishes:** Boire, "On Cognitive Liberty (Part I)," Journal of Cognitive Liberties 1:1 (Winter 1999/2000), 7–13 (series continued through vol. 4, 2003). The coinage's object is mental self-determination against government control of consciousness: emerging technologies (biogenetic modification, human-computer interfacing, brain-scanning, nanotechnology, neural-networking, pharmaceuticals) and the criminalized entheogens (psilocybin, DMT, mescaline, LSD), forced medication and involuntary institutionalization. Consistent with Bublitz 2013's citation ("Boire 2000, p. 8") and Sententia 2004.
- **Key passages:** "the right to control one's own consciousness is the quintessence of freedom" · "each person is free to direct one's own consciousness; one's own underlying mental processes, and one's beliefs, opinions, and worldview" · Mill: "Over himself, over his own body and mind, the individual is sovereign" (quoted by Boire).
