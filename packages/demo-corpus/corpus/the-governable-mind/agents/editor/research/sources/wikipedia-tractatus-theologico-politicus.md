# wikipedia-tractatus-theologico-politicus
- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tractatus_Theologico-Politicus · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 1
- **What it establishes:** False imprint: title page named Hamburg and publisher "Henricus Künraht (who had died in 1605)"; actually Rieuwertsz, Amsterdam, 1670; condemned 1673, "officially banned the following year" (cites Nadler, *A Book Forged in Hell*, 230). Supports C20 (Hamburg imprint).
- **Key passages:** "the title page identified the city of publication as Hamburg and the publisher as Henricus Künraht (who had died in 1605)."
- **Reliability:** Tertiary; used only for a bibliographic detail it sources to Nadler.
- **Relevance to this project:** C20; §I.
