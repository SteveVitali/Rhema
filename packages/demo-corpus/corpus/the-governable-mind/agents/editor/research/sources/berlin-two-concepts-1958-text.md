# berlin-two-concepts-1958-text
- **URL:** https://berlin.wolf.ox.ac.uk/published_works/tcl/tcl-e.pdf (context page https://berlin.wolf.ox.ac.uk/published_works/tcl/) · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 1
- **What it establishes:** The 1958 "Text delivered to the Clarendon Press." Negative-liberty question reads "what he wants to do or be" (matches C40's wording); the positive-liberty passage reads "to bully, oppress, torture them in the name of their 'real' selves" (does NOT match C44's "in the name, and on behalf, of"). Bracketed page numbers ([169], [180]) are from a later edition, not *Four Essays*.
- **Key passages:** "is or should be left to do or be what he wants to do or be, without interference by other persons?" [169]; "to bully, oppress, torture them in the name of their 'real' selves" [180].
- **Reliability:** Primary (Isaiah Berlin Virtual Library, editor Henry Hardy).
- **Relevance to this project:** C40, C44; §IV, §XV.
