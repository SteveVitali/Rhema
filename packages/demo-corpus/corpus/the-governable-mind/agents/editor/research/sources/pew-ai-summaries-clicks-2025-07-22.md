# pew-ai-summaries-clicks-2025-07-22
- **URL:** https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/ · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 (agenda 8)
- **What it establishes:** Chapekis & Lieb, Pew Research Center, July 22, 2025. March 1-31, 2025; 68,879 unique Google searches by 900 U.S. adults; 12,593 produced an AI summary. "Users who encountered an AI summary clicked on a traditional search result link in 8% of all visits"; without a summary "15% of visits." Clicks on a source cited inside the summary: "just 1% of all visits." Sessions ended on "26% of pages with an AI summary, compared with 16% of pages with only traditional search results." Most-cited sources in summaries: Wikipedia, YouTube, Reddit (15% of listed sources).
- **Key passages:** as quoted.
- **Reliability:** Primary empirical study; independent.
- **Relevance to this project:** Agenda 8 (l.194, l.254): the empirical form of "substitution of synthesis for archive" - the source beneath the summary is reached in 1% of visits.
