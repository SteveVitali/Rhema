# firth-rait-ordinance-1643-bho
- **URL:** https://www.british-history.ac.uk/no-series/acts-ordinances-interregnum/pp184-186 · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 1
- **What it establishes:** Primary text of the Ordinance for the Regulating of Printing, 14 June 1643. Confirms both draft quotations verbatim; shows that search/seizure authority was given to the Master and Wardens together with parliamentary officers and the Commons' examiners.
- **Key passages:** 'unless the same be first approved of and licensed … and entred in the Register Book of the Company of Stationers' / 'to make diligent search in all places, where they shall think meet, for all unlicensed Printing Presses'
- **Reliability:** Primary (Firth & Rait 1911 edition via BHO).
- **Relevance to this project:** C47
