# richards-2015-csmonitor-intellectual-privacy
- **URL:** https://www.csmonitor.com/World/Passcode/Passcode-Voices/2015/0225/What-is-intellectual-privacy-and-how-yours-is-being-violated · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 legal/philosophical
- **What it establishes:** Neil Richards, op-ed, Feb. 25, 2015, on the book Intellectual Privacy (OUP 2015). Definition: protection "from being watched and interfered with when we're reading, surfing the Web, talking on the phone, and sending e-mail to confidants"; reading records as intellectual records (the Kindle keeps "detailed records of what we buy, browse, how long our mouse rests over a word"); the mechanism: "we act differently when we're watched. We become boring, inoffensive, and mainstream."
- **Key passages:** as quoted.
- **Reliability:** Primary (author's own summary of the book); the book itself not opened.
- **Relevance to this project:** Agenda 4 (l.78, l.82): the "developmental history of a belief" is Richards's "intellectual records" sharpened from consumption to reasoning; the book, not the 2008 article, is the mature statement and should be the citation.
