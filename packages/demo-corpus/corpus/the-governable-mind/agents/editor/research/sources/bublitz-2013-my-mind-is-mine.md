# bublitz-2013-my-mind-is-mine
- **URL:** https://www.antoniocasella.eu/dnlaw/Bublitz_2013.pdf · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 2
- **What it establishes:** Bublitz, 'My Mind is Mine!? Cognitive Liberty as a Legal Concept' (Springer 2013; draft PDF read in full). Freedom of thought absolute hence construed narrowly; term coined by Boire/Sententia/Blitz; fn. 23 rejects Blitz's extension to devices as collapsing personality into property rights.
- **Key passages:** "Freedom of thought is an absolute right, i.e. there are no limitation clauses" · "In light of its absolute (unrestrictable) nature, freedom of thought has to be construed narrowly" · "this expansion eliminates the distinction between personality and property rights ... freedom of thought can only be meaningfully construed in relation to the human mental realm"
- **Reliability:** Primary scholarly source (author's draft); independent.
- **Relevance to this project:** Report §1.2, §4′(A): the published objection to the draft's central move; draft l.74–86.
