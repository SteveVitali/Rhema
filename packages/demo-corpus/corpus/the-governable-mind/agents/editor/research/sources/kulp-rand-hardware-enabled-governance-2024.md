# kulp-rand-hardware-enabled-governance-2024
- **URL:** https://www.rand.org/pubs/working_papers/WRA3056-1.html · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 policy
- **What it establishes:** Kulp, Gonzales, Smith, Heim, Puri, Vermeer, Winkelman, 'Hardware-Enabled Governance Mechanisms' (RAND WR-A3056-1, Jan 2024; PDF read via text proxy; landing page 403). Offline Licensing (renewable licenses for hardware features tied to GPU serial numbers) and Fixed Set (interconnect limited to preauthorized chips). Explicitly extends HEMs to consumer-grade chips and consumer products; neither mechanism checks which model runs; minimal privacy/civil-liberties discussion. This is the paper the agenda calls 'Kulveit et al.'
- **Key passages:** 'HEMs could further secure consumer-grade chips from misuse. ... consumer chips can still be used for frontier AI development or inference' (p. 7) · 'If such HEMs were incorporated into consumer products, they could obviate the need for additional export controls on consumer GPUs' (pp. 9–10) · 'Consumer devices could be equipped with Fixed Set mechanisms to prevent their aggregation into a supercomputer' (p. 10).
- **Reliability:** Primary; RAND working paper; national-security framing.
- **Relevance to this project:** Q4: l.286 false for Kulp; Offline Licensing on a consumer GPU is 'circumvention disables acceleration' (l.280) in all but name.
