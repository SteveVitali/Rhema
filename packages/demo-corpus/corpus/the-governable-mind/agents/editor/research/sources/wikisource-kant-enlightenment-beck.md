# wikisource-kant-enlightenment-beck
- **URL:** https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/What_is_Enlightenment%3F · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 1
- **What it establishes:** Beck (1963) translation. Two of the draft's four Kant fragments are Beck's wording, not Gregor's: "as a scholar before the reading public"; "in a particular civil post or office which is entrusted to him." Beck renders the opening as "self-incurred tutelage."
- **Key passages:** "By the public use of one's reason I understand the use which a person makes of it as a scholar before the reading public. Private use I call that which one may make of it in a particular civil post or office which is entrusted to him."
- **Reliability:** Primary (translation), public domain.
- **Relevance to this project:** C25; §I.
