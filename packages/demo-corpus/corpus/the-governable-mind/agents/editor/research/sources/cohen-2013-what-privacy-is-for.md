# cohen-2013-what-privacy-is-for
- **URL:** https://harvardlawreview.org/print/vol-126/what-privacy-is-for/ · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 2
- **What it establishes:** Cohen, 126 Harv. L. Rev. 1904 (2013): privacy shelters emergent subjectivity from being rendered fixed, transparent, predictable; surveillance as modulation; freedom from surveillance foundational to reflective citizenship; privacy shelters play from which innovation emerges.
- **Key passages:** privacy "shelters dynamic, emergent subjectivity from the efforts of commercial and government actors to render individuals and communities fixed, transparent, and predictable" · "Freedom from surveillance, whether public or private, is foundational to the practice of informed and reflective citizenship."
- **Reliability:** Primary scholarly source; independent.
- **Relevance to this project:** Report §3, §7; draft l.86–92, l.164 ('modulation').
