# guard-act-s3062-reported-text-2026-05-11
- **URL:** https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119s3062rs/pdf/BILLS-119s3062rs.pdf (reported version, Calendar No. 406; introduced version BILLS-119s3062is also fetched) · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2 review, Phase 2 (agenda 3)
- **What it establishes:** "Guidelines for User Age-verification and Responsible Dialogue Act of 2025" (GUARD Act), S. 3062, introduced Oct. 28, 2025 by Hawley with Blumenthal, Britt, Warner, Murphy, Kelly, Gallego, Lee, Lankford, Cotton, Welch, Hassan, Cortez Masto, Kaine, Gillibrand, Ricketts, Blackburn, Whitehouse, Durbin, Coons; reported by Grassley with an amendment in the nature of a substitute, May 11, 2026 (Judiciary vote 22-0 on April 30, 2026 per press). Scope: "artificial intelligence chatbot" = any service that "produces new expressive content or responses not fully predetermined" and "accepts open-ended natural-language or multimodal user input," excluding narrow-purpose bots; so general-purpose assistants are covered. Sec. 5(a): "A covered entity shall require each individual accessing an artificial intelligence chatbot to make a user account in order to use or otherwise interact with such chatbot." Sec. 5(b)(1): existing accounts must be frozen on the effective date and restored only after "age data that is verifiable using a reasonable age verification process"; (b)(2) new accounts verified at creation; (b)(3) "periodically review previously verified user accounts"; (b)(5) verification data minimized, encrypted, retained "no longer than is reasonably necessary," not shared or sold. Sec. 3(5): "reasonable age verification measure" = "a method that is authenticated to relate to a user ... such as (A) a government-issued identification; or (B) any other commercially reasonable method that can reliably and accurately (i) determine whether a user is an adult"; self-attestation or birth date entry "is not sufficient." Sec. 6: minors barred from "AI companions." Sec. 5(c): disclosure of non-human and non-professional status at conversation start and every 30 minutes. Sec. 4: new 18 U.S.C. ch. 6 criminal offenses for chatbots that solicit minors sexually or promote suicide/violence ($100,000 per offense). Enforcement by the Attorney General and state AGs; civil penalty $100,000 per violation. No logging or content-retention mandate; no requirement to link verified identity to conversation content.
- **Key passages:** as quoted above (Secs. 3(5), 5(a), 5(b)(1)-(3), 6).
- **Reliability:** Primary (bill text as reported).
- **Relevance to this project:** Agenda 3. This is a liberal-state proposal of mandatory accounts plus age verification (government ID or equivalent) for every user of every general-purpose chatbot, i.e., a layer-3 identity gate in all but name, reported out of committee unanimously. It supplies the bill number for l.282 ("Powerful inference requires an identity, because accountability requires a name") and bears on l.276/l.290 ("first datum").
