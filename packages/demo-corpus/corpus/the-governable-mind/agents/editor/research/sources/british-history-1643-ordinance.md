# british-history-1643-ordinance
- **URL:** https://www.british-history.ac.uk/no-series/acts-ordinances-interregnum/pp184-186 · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 review, Phase 1
- **What it establishes:** The June 14, 1643 Ordinance authorizes "the Master and Wardens of the said Company" (Stationers) with others "to make diligent search in all places" and seize unlicensed presses — the basis for "licensing administered through a guild" (C24; Part C's C47).
- **Key passages:** "the Master and Wardens of the said Company ... are hereby Authorized and required, from time to time, to make diligent search in all places".
- **Reliability:** Primary (Firth & Rait 1911 edition of the Ordinance).
- **Relevance to this project:** C24, C47; §I, §VI.
