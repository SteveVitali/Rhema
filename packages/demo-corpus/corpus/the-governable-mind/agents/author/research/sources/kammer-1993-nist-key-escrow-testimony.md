# kammer-1993-nist-key-escrow-testimony
- **URL:** https://www.globalsecurity.org/intell/library/congress/1993_hr/clipper.htm · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 drafting, claims C31
- **What it establishes:** Testimony of Raymond G. Kammer, NIST Deputy Director, June 17, 1993, on key escrow encryption: two escrow holders, 80-bit Skipjack, and NIST's early retirement of the 'Clipper Chip' name.
- **Key passages:** "Law enforcement can then take the serial number and a certification of their legal authorization to two escrow holders". "The SKIPJACK algorithm with an 80 bit long cryptographic key is approximately 16 million times stronger than DES." "we have chosen to discontinue to use of the term 'Clipper Chip'"
- **Reliability:** Primary (congressional testimony) via a mirror (GlobalSecurity.org); reliable transcript but not the official record.
- **Relevance to this project:** C31: minor colour — the government itself dropped the 'Clipper' name within two months.
