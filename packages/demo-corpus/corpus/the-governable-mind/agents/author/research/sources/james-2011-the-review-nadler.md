- **What it establishes:** Susan James (Birkbeck; author of *Spinoza on Philosophy, Religion, and Politics*), review of Steven Nadler, *A Book Forged in Hell*, *Times Higher Education*, 24 November 2011. States the TTP was banned by the States of Holland in July 1674.
- **Key passages:**
  - "Within a few months of its publication in 1670, the identity of its author became known, and a storm of opprobrium broke over Spinoza's head."
  - "in July 1674 it was eventually banned by the States of Holland. According to the States' counsellors, it was a 'blasphemous and soul-harming book, full of groundless and dangerous opinions.'"
- **Reliability:** Secondary (review by a specialist scholar summarizing Nadler); independent of Wikipedia. Does not mention the co-banned books (Hobbes, Meyer).
- **Relevance to this project:** C56 — the 1674 ban, with the issuing body (States of Holland, not the States-General).
