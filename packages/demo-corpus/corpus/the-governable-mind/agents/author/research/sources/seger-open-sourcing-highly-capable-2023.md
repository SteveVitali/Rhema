# seger-open-sourcing-highly-capable-2023
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09227 (PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.09227; arXiv stamp reads "v1 [cs.CY] 29 Sep 2023"; arXiv ID indicates Nov 2023 listing; 50 pp.) · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 response round, open-sourcing / staged release
- **What it establishes:** GovAI report arguing that for some near-future highly capable foundation models, open-sourcing's risks may outweigh benefits, so such models "should not be open-sourced, at least not initially." Acknowledges the benefits of openness and canvasses alternatives (staged release, gated download, research API, structured access). Printed page = PDF page. Publisher's preferred citation is printed on p. 1.
- **Key passages:**
  - Abstract, p. 1: "This offers advantages such as enabling external oversight, accelerating progress, and decentralizing control over AI development and use."
  - Abstract, p. 1: "In such a case, highly capable foundation models should not be open-sourced, at least not initially. Alternative strategies, including non-open-source model sharing options, are explored."
  - Sec. 6 Conclusion, p. 34: "Open-sourcing offers clear advantages including enabling external oversight, accelerating progress, and decentralizing control over a potentially transformative technology."
  - Sec. 6, p. 34: "how alternative model sharing options (e.g. staged release, gated access, and research API) might further some of the same goals as open-sourcing."
  - Sec. 6, p. 34: "The goal of this paper is therefore not to argue that foundation model development should be kept behind closed doors. Model sharing, including open-sourcing, remains a valuable practice in most cases."
  - Sec. 4.1 table, p. 17 ("Alternative methods"): "Grant privileged model access to trusted (independently selected) third-party auditors via gated-download or research API." / "Explore social impacts and safety issues through incremental, staged release of models."
  - Sec. 2.2, p. 8 (paraphrase): structured access = model access mediated e.g. through an API (cites Shevlane 2022).
- **Reliability:** Primary. Centre for the Governance of AI report with 21 authors; safety-community perspective (GovAI); authorship "does not entail endorsement of all claims."
- **Relevance to this project:** Establishes the "not initially / staged release" position the essay engages with.
