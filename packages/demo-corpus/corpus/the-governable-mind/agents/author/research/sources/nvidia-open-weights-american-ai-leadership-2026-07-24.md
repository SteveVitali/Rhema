- **What it establishes:** Coalition letter dated July 24, 2026. Defines open-weight models as "AI models that anyone can download, inspect, modify, and run on their own infrastructure." Contains the defenders sentence. Asks policymakers to expand compute access, invest in shared training assets, and keep "the frontier plural by avoiding premature restrictions on open models"; defends distillation. Signatory block (~250 names) includes OpenAI, Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Meta, Mistral, Cohere, Hugging Face, Palantir, SpaceX, Andreessen Horowitz, Y Combinator, Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike, NVIDIA. "Anthropic" does not appear anywhere in the document (grep count 0).
- **Key passages:**
  - "Open weight models—AI models that anyone can download, inspect, modify, and run on their own infrastructure—are an important part of that foundation"
  - "In a world where cybersecurity attackers use advanced AI, defenders need access to models with comparable capabilities so they can detect, simulate, and respond to emerging threats."
  - "Once released, the weights are beyond the original developer's control, and modified versions are difficult to trace or reverse. But the right response to this risk is not to prohibit open weights."
  - "keeping the frontier plural by avoiding premature restrictions on open models that stifle competition or drive innovation overseas"
- **Reliability:** Primary (advocacy letter); industry-interested.
