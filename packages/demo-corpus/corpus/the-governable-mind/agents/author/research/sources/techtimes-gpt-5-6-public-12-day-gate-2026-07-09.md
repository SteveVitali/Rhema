## Added 2026-09-16 (v2→v3 round, slice K, K12; page re-read)
- Arithmetic: June 26 → July 9, 2026 is 13 days elapsed (date subtraction). TechTimes's "12 days" ("For the 12 days between June 26 and July 9") counts only the days strictly between the two dates (June 27–July 8). The earlier line in this note, "12 days, 13 counting inclusively", is wrong as arithmetic: exclusive-of-both = 12; elapsed = 13; inclusive-of-both = 14. "Thirteen days later" / "thirteen days after the preview" is correct; a bare "thirteen-day hold" is defensible but a reader holding TechTimes will see "12-day". Safest: "thirteen days after the preview" or "about two weeks".
- July 9 public release independently confirmed this round by Fortune (Aug 4): "its July 9 debut of GPT-5.6" (sources/fortune-white-house-framework-unpublished-2026-08-04.md).
