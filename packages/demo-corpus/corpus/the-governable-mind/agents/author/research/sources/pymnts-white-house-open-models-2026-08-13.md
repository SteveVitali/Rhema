- **What it establishes:** Aug 13, 2026. WIRED reported the White House is preparing to include "powerful open-weight AI models" in the voluntary pre-release cybersecurity testing framework once they reach "frontier-level capabilities" — reversing a position "reportedly shared with AI companies earlier this month" that "the voluntary review process would not apply to U.S. open-weight models." Framework established by the June EO; White House fact sheet says developers of "covered frontier models" may "provide the federal government with early access for cybersecurity evaluation before broader release."
