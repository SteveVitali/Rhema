# multistate-montana-right-to-compute
- **URL:** https://www.multistate.ai/updates/vol-59-montana-right-to-compute-law · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 response round, Finding J
- **What it establishes:** MultiState summary of SB 212, signed April 16, 2025. Quotes the right ("fundamental right to privately own and use computational resources for lawful purposes"), describes the "narrowly tailored ... compelling government interest" standard as strict scrutiny, "the highest standard of review," and reports that the original bill's requirement that deployers have "the capability to disable the artificial intelligence system's control over the infrastructure and revert to human control within a reasonable amount of time" at critical infrastructure was removed before passage, leaving a risk-management-policy requirement.
- **Key passages:**
  - "Any government restriction on computational resources must be narrowly tailored to fulfill a compelling government interest."
  - removed provision: "the capability to disable the artificial intelligence system's control over the infrastructure and revert to human control within a reasonable amount of time"
- **Reliability:** Secondary, state-policy tracker.
- **Relevance to this project:** Establishes what was stripped: the shutdown mandate. Pair with the enrolled text.
