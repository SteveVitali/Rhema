# wang-preservation-order-2025-05-13
- **URL:** https://cdn.arstechnica.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/NYT-v-OpenAI-Preservation-Order-5-13-25.pdf · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v2→v3 response round, slice L
- **What it establishes:** PRIMARY. In re OpenAI, Inc., Copyright Infringement Litigation, 25-md-3143 (SHS)(OTW), Order of Magistrate Judge Ona T. Wang dated May 13, 2025 (2 pp., read in full). Earlier (Jan 2025) she had DENIED "wholesale preservation"; on renewal she ordered preservation of output log data that would otherwise be deleted, going forward, "until further order of the Court." With the Sept 26, 2025 end date (see engadget-openai-preservation-lifted and openai-response-to-nyt-data-demands), the forward obligation ran May 13 → Sept 26, 2025 = 136 days, i.e. about four and a half months.
- **Key passages:**
  - p. 2: "Accordingly, OpenAI is NOW DIRECTED to preserve and segregate all output log data that would otherwise be deleted on a going forward basis until further order of the Court (in essence, the output log data that OpenAI has been destroying), whether such data might be deleted at a user’s request or because of “numerous privacy laws and regulations” that might require OpenAI to do so."
  - p. 1: "I denied the News Plaintiffs’ request for “wholesale preservation of the output log data.”"
  - p. 1 (Wang's question at the Jan 22, 2025 conference): "Is there a way to segregate the data for the users that have expressly asked for their chat logs to be deleted, or is there a way to anonymize ..."
- **Reliability:** Primary court order (Ars Technica-hosted copy).
