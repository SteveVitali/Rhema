# wikipedia-lodewijk-meyer
- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodewijk_Meyer · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C56 (lead only)
- **What it establishes:** Tertiary statement that Meyer's *Philosophia S. Scripturae Interpres* "had been banned by the Court of Holland together with Spinoza's Tractatus Theologico-Politicus in 1674." Web-search snippets (Brill's *Quaerendo* article on the Dutch TTP translations, not openable — 403) add that the same 19 July 1674 placard covered Hobbes's *Leviathan* and the *Bibliotheca Fratrum Polonorum*; I could not open that article, so treat the co-banned list as unverified.
- **Key passages:**
  - "which had been banned by the Court of Holland together with Spinoza's Tractatus Theologico-Politicus in 1674."
- **Reliability:** Tertiary; lead only. The "Court of Holland" (Hof van Holland) vs "States of Holland" wording differs between sources: the States recommended, the Court issued the placard (consistent with Gordon's "officials of the Dutch court carried out the recommendation of the States of Holland").
- **Relevance to this project:** C56 — only if the essay wants the detail that the TTP was banned alongside *Leviathan*; that detail needs a further source before use.
