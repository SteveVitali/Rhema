(The 2608 identifier and the 1 Jul stamp do not match each other; cite as arXiv:2608.07514 / TMLR 2026 and avoid a day-level date.) The "6 to 12 months" figure is NOT the paper's own measurement; it is a citation to Cottier (2024) and Maslej et al. (2025). The paper is a research agenda (16 open technical challenges) and proposes NO capability or compute threshold for release; "threshold" appears only in the two Introduction sentences quoted below.
- **Key passages:**
  - Intro: "Simultaneously, proprietary model developers have reported that their models are approaching critical risk thresholds (Google AI, 2025; Anthropic, 2025; OpenAI, 2025a). Meanwhile current work estimates that the capabilities of frontier open-weight models only lag 6 to 12 months behind (Cottier, 2024; Maslej et al., 2025). This suggests that open-weight models could soon cross critical capability thresholds."
  - Later (staged deployment, "Significance"): "research showing open-weight model capabilities to consistently be only 6-12 months behind frontier proprietary models (Cottier, 2024; Maslej et al., 2025)."
  - Abstract: "managing their risks is also challenging because they can be modified arbitrarily, used without oversight, and spread irreversibly." · "we present 16 open technical challenges for open-weight model safety involving training data, training algorithms, evaluations, deployment, and ecosystem monitoring."
- **Reliability:** Primary, peer-reviewed (TMLR); authors include UK AISI staff and a Hugging Face researcher. The lag figure is secondhand and rests on 2024–25 data; AISI's July 2026 cyber measurement (4 to 7 months) is the fresher number.
- **Relevance to this project:** Verifies the wording; the essay must attribute "6 to 12 months" to the estimates Casper et al. cite, not to a Casper et al. finding, and must not say the paper proposes a threshold.
