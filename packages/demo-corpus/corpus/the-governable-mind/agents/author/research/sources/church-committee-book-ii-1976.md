- **What it establishes:** Final Report, Book II, 'Intelligence Activities and the Rights of Americans', S. Rep. No. 94-755, 94th Cong., 2d Sess., dated April 26, 1976 (404-page scan hosted by the Senate Intelligence Committee). Contains the committee's headline finding on the scope of domestic surveillance abuse.
- **Key passages:** Book II, p. 5 (PDF p. 21), 'Summary of the Main Problem': "Too many people have been spied upon by too many Government agencies and too much information has been collected." (OCR reads 'to much ... beeen'; the printed text is as quoted.) Continues: "The Government has often undertaken the secret surveillance of citizens on the basis of their political beliefs, even when those beliefs posed no threat of violence". Transmittal letter: "The root cause of the excesses which our record amply demonstrates has been failure to apply the wisdom of the constitutional system of checks and balances to intelligence activities."
