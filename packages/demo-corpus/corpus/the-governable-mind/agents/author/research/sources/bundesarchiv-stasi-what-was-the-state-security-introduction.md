# bundesarchiv-stasi-what-was-the-state-security-introduction
- **URL:** https://www.bundesarchiv.de/en/stasi-records-archive/education/what-was-the-state-security/introduction/ · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 drafting, claims C36
- **What it establishes:** Bundesarchiv (Stasi Records Archive) English introduction: the MfS penetrated private life and bugged phones; about 91,000 full-time employees by 1989; HV A figures. Does not itemise telex, room surveillance, technical observation, or the evolution of interception technology.
- **Key passages:** "The MfS penetrated into the citizens' private lives; observing them, bugging their phones, spying on them, arresting and interrogating them." "By 1989 the State Security had about 91,000 full-time employees." "By 1989 the HV A had a full-time staff of 4,600, plus 13,400 unofficial collaborators in the GDR and another 1,500 in West Germany."
- **Reliability:** Primary/official (German Federal Archives).
- **Relevance to this project:** C36: the 91,000 figure; the outline over-attributes the detailed surveillance taxonomy to this page.
