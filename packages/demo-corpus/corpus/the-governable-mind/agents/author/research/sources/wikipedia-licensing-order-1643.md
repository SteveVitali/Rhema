- **What it establishes:** Tertiary summary: ordinance of 14 June 1643 titled "An Ordinance for the Regulating of Printing"; the Stationers' Company "was given the responsibility of acting as censor, in return for a monopoly of the printing trade"; Milton's *Areopagitica* published November 1644 in response, full title "Areopagitica: A speech of Mr. John Milton for the Liberty of Unlicenc'd Printing, to the Parlament of England."
- **Key passages:**
  - "The Stationers' Company was given the responsibility of acting as censor, in return for a monopoly of the printing trade."
  - Sole citation for the Stationers' claims: Eunmi Park, "Areopagitica in the Licensing Controversy: Milton's Rhetorical Strategies and Modes" (PDF).
