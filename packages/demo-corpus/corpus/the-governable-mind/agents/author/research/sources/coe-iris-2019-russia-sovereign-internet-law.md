# coe-iris-2019-russia-sovereign-internet-law
- **URL:** https://merlin.obs.coe.int/article/8603 · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 drafting, claims C37
- **What it establishes:** Andrei Richter, 'Sovereign Internet Law adopted', IRIS 2019-6:1/22 (European Audiovisual Observatory, Council of Europe): Federal Law No. 90-FZ signed by Putin on 1 May 2019, in force 1 November 2019; ISPs must install DPI-capable devices; Roskomnadzor to monitor threats and issue instructions through them.
- **Key passages:** "signed by President Vladimir Putin on 1 May 2019"; "enters into force on 1 November 2019 (with the exception of some provisions)"; "requires ISPs to install devices enabling DPI ('Deep Packet Inspection'...in their networks"; "Roskomnadzor will monitor threats to Russia's Internet access and transmit (via special devices) instructions to ISPs about the countering of such threats"
- **Reliability:** Secondary but institutional (Council of Europe legal observatory), written by a media-law scholar; neutral.
- **Relevance to this project:** C37: the law number and dates.
