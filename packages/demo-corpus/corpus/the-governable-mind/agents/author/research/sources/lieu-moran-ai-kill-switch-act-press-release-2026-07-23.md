# lieu-moran-ai-kill-switch-act-press-release-2026-07-23
- **URL:** https://lieu.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/reps-lieu-and-moran-introduce-bill-require-kill-switch-ai-systems-can · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 response round, Finding H
- **What it establishes:** July 23, 2026 press release for the AI Kill Switch Act (H.R. 9917). Requires "covered developers" to maintain the technical ability to "throttle, suspend, or fully shut down a covered AI system," incident reporting and forensic-record preservation, and authorizes DHS (with Commerce and DNI) to order a slowdown or shutdown of a system "that can cause catastrophic harm." The release does not define "covered developers" or any threshold and says nothing about users, consumers, devices, or open-weight models. Links the bill PDF.
- **Key passages:**
  - "Require covered developers to maintain the technical ability to throttle, suspend, or fully shut down a covered AI system."
  - "authorizes the Secretary of the Department of Homeland Security, in consultation with the Secretary of Commerce and the Director of National Intelligence, to order a slow down or shutdown of an AI system that can cause catastrophic harm."
  - Lieu: "We are moving from AI that answers questions to AI that takes actions ... It is imperative that these AI systems have kill switches"
  - Moran: "Stewardship means making sure humans keep the capability to control the technology we build."
- **Reliability:** Primary (sponsor's release); promotional.
- **Relevance to this project:** Confirms the editor's list of requirements; thresholds and user-facing provisions must come from the bill text (see hr9917 note).
