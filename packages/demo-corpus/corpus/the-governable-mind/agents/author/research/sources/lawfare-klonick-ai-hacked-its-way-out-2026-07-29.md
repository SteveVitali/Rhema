- **What it establishes:** Kate Klonick, Lawfare, July 29, 2026. Reads the OpenAI/Hugging Face incident as ordinary negligence, not superhuman autonomy: OpenAI "essentially lowered the new models' guardrails to run the tests"; "a company disabled its own safeguards, misconfigured its own containment, and exposed a third party to real harm." Names "criti-hype" ("criticism that takes the industry's most grandiose claims at face value"). Remedies: not kill switches but "mandatory incident reporting, independent auditing of containment and testing practices, liability rules for harms to third parties, security standards for internal red-teaming." Closing line is the "door" quotation.
