# techtimes-claude-identity-verification-2026-06-21
- **URL:** https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318778/20260621/claude-identity-verification-starts-july-8-what-facial-data-anthropic-collects.htm · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 response round, Finding A (Persona verification)
- **What it establishes:** June 21, 2026. Verification via Persona Identities begins July 8, 2026 for Claude Free/Pro/Max (Team, Enterprise, API exempt). Collects government-ID image, live selfie photo/video, and "facial geometry templates" that "may be considered biometric data in some jurisdictions." Verification testing began in April 2026, predating the June 12 shutdown. Retention limits unpublished. Explicitly frames the biometric system as only "one part of the solution" to nationality compliance and says whether it satisfies a future nationality requirement "depends on how the ongoing White House-Anthropic negotiations resolve."
- **Key passages:**
  - "The July 8 biometric framework gives Anthropic one part of the solution: a mechanism to confirm that a user is who they say they are."
  - "whether it can satisfy a future nationality-compliance requirement depends on how the ongoing White House-Anthropic negotiations resolve."
  - "What those retention limits are has not been published."
  - Anthropic "has contractually restricted Persona from using verification data for advertising, marketing, or model training."
- **Reliability:** Secondary, tech press; this piece describes tiers rather than the "flagged subset" nuance reported by TechCrunch/Gizmodo/TNW.
- **Relevance to this project:** Supports "biometric identity verification through Persona ... from July 8" for consumer tiers, but see the flagged-subset caveat in the TechCrunch/Gizmodo/TNW notes.
