# biometricupdate-openai-hardware-passkeys-2026-07-14
- **URL:** https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/openai-requires-hardware-backed-passkeys-for-trusted-cyber-access · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 response round, Finding D (what identity is required)
- **What it establishes:** July 14, 2026. Quotes OpenAI: TAC individual verification involves a "government ID check, know-your-customer (KYC) identity checks, and additional trust signals such as device health," "conducted via Persona to help OpenAI determine whether an account belongs to a real user engaged in legitimate cybersecurity work." From Sept 1, 2026 TAC members must "authenticate with a hardware-backed passkey to retain access to frontier models"; non-compliant accounts "return to the standard level of model access."
- **Key passages:**
  - "government ID check, know-your-customer (KYC) identity checks, and additional trust signals such as device health"
  - "conducted via Persona to help OpenAI determine whether an account belongs to a real user engaged in legitimate cybersecurity work"

- **Relevance to this project:** Establishes that OpenAI's TAC requires a government ID via Persona, the same vendor Anthropic uses.
