# willison-openai-preservation-order
- **URL:** https://simonwillison.net/2025/Oct/23/openai-no-longer-has-to-preserve/ and https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jun/5/openai-court-order/ · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 response round, item 1
- **What it establishes:** The June 5, 2025 post pins the original preservation order to May 13, 2025 (linking the two-page order PDF hosted by Ars Technica) and quotes its operative paragraph; it notes the order covered "temporary private chats and API outputs served to paying customers, which previously had a 30 day retention policy." The Oct. 23, 2025 post relays Engadget: Wang's Oct. 9 order lifts the going-forward duty; already-preserved logs and NYT-flagged accounts remain. Willison calls the original order "very worrying" and the lifting "a relief."
- **Key passages:** From the May 13 order as quoted (June 5 post): "OpenAI is NOW DIRECTED to preserve and segregate all output log data that would otherwise be deleted on a going forward basis until further order of the Court (in essence, the output log data that OpenAI has been destroying)". / Oct. 23 post: "OpenAI were compelled by a court order to preserve all output, even from private chats, in case it became relevant to the ongoing New York Times lawsuit."
- **Reliability:** Secondary (independent technologist's link blog) quoting the primary order verbatim; the June post links the order PDF itself.
- **Relevance to this project:** Supplies the May 13, 2025 date and the order's operative language; corroborates the Oct. 9 lifting.
