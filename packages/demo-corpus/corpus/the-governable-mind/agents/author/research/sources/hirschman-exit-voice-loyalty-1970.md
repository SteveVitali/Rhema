# hirschman-exit-voice-loyalty-1970
- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exit,_Voice,_and_Loyalty · **Accessed:** 2026-09-16 · **For:** v1 response round, exit/voice citation
- **What it establishes:** Bibliographic details and a short statement of the exit/voice distinction: members of a declining organization can exit (withdraw) or use voice (complain, propose change); loyalty modulates the choice.
- **Key passages:**
  - Wikipedia summary: members "may **exit** (withdraw from the relationship) or employ **voice** (attempt improvement through communication of complaints and proposals)."
  - Hirschman, p. 16 (as quoted by Wikipedia): "Voice can be graduated, all the way from faint grumbling to violent protest."
- **Reliability:** Tertiary (Wikipedia) for the framing; the p. 16 quotation is attributed there to the primary text. Sufficient for a reference-list entry, not for a contested claim.
- **Relevance to this project:** Supplies the citation for the essay's exit/voice usage. Reference: Albert O. Hirschman, *Exit, Voice, and Loyalty: Responses to Decline in Firms, Organizations, and States* (Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1970), ISBN 0-674-27660-4.
