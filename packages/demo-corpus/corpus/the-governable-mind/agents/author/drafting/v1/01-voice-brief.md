## Exemplar excerpts kept in view while writing

> intelligence. It is that the intelligence of the participant does not

## Signature moves to use here

- **Refuse the obvious word, then coin.** The prologue refuses "AI regulation"
  as the subject; §III refuses "mind reading"; §VIII refuses "America is
  China"; §IX refuses "Newspeak."
- **Definition by contrast pair**: offloading / extended; interference /
  domination; capability / use; archive / synthesis; governing power /
  governing deliberation.
- **Intellectual history run forward**, with the through-line audible in a
  one-sentence paragraph every few hundred words ("What leaves the skull
  enters the world.").
- **The objection gets its own section, announced as owed** (§X: "The
  strongest objection to everything above now deserves its full force").
- **State the method's limit before using it**: "What follows is a
  reconstruction, not a history. There was no four-century march from
  Spinoza to local inference."
- **Apply the essay's test to the essay** — briefly, in §XII or the coda.
- **The epigraph's key term returns in the last sentence.**
- **Evidentiary hedging as a register** in §§Prologue, VIII, X: "by OpenAI's
  own account"; "as the FT reports"; "no public evidence establishes"; dated
  provisional claims ("as of September 2026").

## Anti-signature list (ration explicitly)

- **Em dashes.** The long referenced form runs 0.6–1.4/1k. Use parentheses
  and colons instead. Hard cap 1.4/1k (~16 in 11,500 words).
- **Negation-then-correction monotony.** The blind check's tell: "not X. It is
  Y." four times in five paragraphs. Rotate the three forms; place at
  paragraph hinges; let documents and definitions carry other paragraphs.
- **The aphoristic closer** on every section — cap at one per section, and
  some sections end plainly.
- **Triads stacked back to back** — none.
- **"This is why"** — at most 2–3 in the piece.
- **Rhetorical questions** — only in structural positions (the prologue's
  prior question; a section-closing question the next section answers; the
  seven test questions in §XII, which are the outline's form).
- **Warm, quotable temperature sentence after sentence** — the blind check's
  other tell. The real register is cooler and plainer; let a paragraph end
  on a fact.
- **Escalation without limitation** — "Nothing in it doubts itself." Each
  major claim carries its scope limit.

## First-person policy

None. The persona's long form is at zero authorial *I*; the author's
experience is not available to this agent and is not invented. Authorial
commitment is carried by "this essay" and by third-person address to
"readers." No anecdote, no scene not sourced to a document, no "a friend
told me."
