## Flags and escalations (first, always)

Testing at the second layer is constitutionally tolerable if the tester's classifications are inspectable and appealable; if the threshold is defined by what a model can do and never by what its users ask; if there is more than one accredited tester, a published standard, and a sunset; and if enforcement stops at release and never reaches the fourth layer.
