# GAO — federal law enforcement use of commercial facial recognition

- GAO-24-107372: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-24-107372 ; GAO-23-105607: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105607
- Accessed 2026-08-21 (Phase 1). PRIMARY.

Establishes: seven DOJ/DHS agencies used commercial FRT (FBI and ICE-HSI among them);
"about 60,000 searches... when they did not have training requirements in place"
(Oct. 2019–Mar. 2022 = 2.5 years); all seven initially used the services without staff
training requirements.
