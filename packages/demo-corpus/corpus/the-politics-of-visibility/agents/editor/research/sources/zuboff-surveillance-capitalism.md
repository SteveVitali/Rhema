# Zuboff — behavioral surplus & instrumentarian power

- **URLs:** interview read at https://findingctrl.nesta.org.uk/text/shoshana-zuboff/ ; supporting search results incl. review https://www.euppublishing.com/doi/full/10.3366/jspp.2022.0034
- **Accessed:** 2026-08-21

**Establishes:** "Behavioral surplus" = data "with substantial predictive value that exceeds what is required for product or service improvement." "Instrumentarian power" = "the power to know and shape human behavior toward others' ends," which Zuboff explicitly distinguishes from totalitarianism (it works "through the automated medium" of networked devices, not "armaments and armies, terror and murder") and from state surveillance (a market logic, not state security). Relevance to the coinage check: the draft's "behavioral legibility" is state-facing (what the state can retrieve) where behavioral surplus is market-facing (what firms can monetize); the draft's public-private assemblage is precisely the junction Zuboff's framework kept apart. Distinguishable — but must be distinguished on the page.
