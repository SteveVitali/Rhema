# CRS — domestic terrorism law (LSB10340)

- https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/LSB/PDF/LSB10340/LSB10340.2.pdf (PDF text-extracted)
- Accessed 2026-08-21 (Phase 1). PRIMARY.

Establishes verbatim: "Existing federal law defines domestic terrorism but does not
outlaw it by name" (§2331(5)); FTO material support "has no similarly captioned
counterpart on the domestic side"; "Beneath the surface, co-conspirator and accomplice
liability look much like providing material support."; §2339A (predicate-crime support)
vs. §2339B (designated FTOs).
