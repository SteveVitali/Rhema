# Holder v. Humanitarian Law Project, 561 U.S. 1 (2010)

- Syllabus: https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/08-1498.ZS.html ; Opinion: https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/08-1498.ZO.html
- Accessed 2026-08-21 (Phase 1). PRIMARY.

Establishes: "service" covers "advocacy performed in coordination with, or at the
direction of, a foreign terrorist organization"; independent advocacy not prohibited;
§V.C dictum verbatim: "We also do not suggest that Congress could extend the same
prohibition on material support at issue here to domestic organizations."; upheld §2339B
as applied to training/coordinated advocacy for PKK/LTTE.
