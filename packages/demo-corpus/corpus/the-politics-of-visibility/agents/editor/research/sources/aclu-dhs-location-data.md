# ACLU — DHS purchase of app location data (2022-07-18)

- https://www.aclu.org/news/privacy-technology/new-records-detail-dhs-purchase-and-use-of-vast-quantities-of-cell-phone-location-data
- Accessed 2026-08-21 (Phase 1).

Establishes: CBP, ICE, Secret Service, Coast Guard, DHS HQ bought broker location data;
Venntel marketing verbatim: "identify repeat visitors, frequented locations, pinpoint
known associates, and discover pattern of life"; "more than 15 billion location points
from over 250 million cell phones and other mobile devices every day"; data "quietly
extracted from smartphone apps" (Venntel, Babel Street).
