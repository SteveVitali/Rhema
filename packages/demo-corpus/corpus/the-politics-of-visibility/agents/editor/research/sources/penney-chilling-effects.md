**Establishes:** Both the best empirical support beyond Stoycheff (post-Snowden decline in privacy-sensitive Wikipedia browsing) and the honest problem: chilling-effects theory has a long skeptical literature — per the search record, Schauer (1978) conceded its empirical assumptions were "most likely unprovable"; Blasi called it "oft-criticized" "crude behavioral speculation"; Kendrick calls the empirical basis "weak"/"flimsy"; Penney himself concedes conventional fear-based accounts are "narrow and empirically weak" and rebuilds the concept as social conformity. The draft's §X concedes the AJPS non-monotonicity but not this deeper line; its coda's confident mechanism ("the meeting is simply one person smaller") outruns the evidence it cites.
