# Bogost & Warzel, "American Panopticon" (The Atlantic, 2025)

- **URL:** read via mirror https://www.rsn.org/001/american-panopticon.html (orig. theatlantic.com, spring 2025)
- **Accessed:** 2026-08-21

**Establishes:** The highest-profile 2025 magazine treatment of the capacity thesis, aimed at DOGE's federal data consolidation. "America already has all the technology it needs to build a draconian surveillance society — the conditions for such a dystopia have been falling into place slowly over time." "A fragile combination of decades-old laws, norms, and jungly bureaucracy has so far prevented repositories such as these from assembling into a centralized American surveillance state." Catalogues IRS/DHS/DEA/VA databases, 640M+ photo face systems, phone location data; hypothesizes weaponization against critics. The draft's differentiators vs. this piece: private infrastructure (Flock/brokers/Palantir) rather than intra-government consolidation; the classification layer; the philosophy; the falsification list.
