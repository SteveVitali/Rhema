# Glissant, "For Opacity" (Poetics of Relation, 1990/1997)

- **URL:** chapter PDF read at https://sites.evergreen.edu/politicalshakespeares/wp-content/uploads/sites/226/2015/12/Glissant-For-Opacity.pdf
- **Accessed:** 2026-08-21 (full chapter read)

**Establishes:** The term collision. "We clamor for the right to opacity for everyone." Glissant's opacity is epistemic and anti-colonial — "it is impossible to reduce anyone, no matter who, to a truth he would not have generated on his own"; "widespread consent to specific opacities is the most straightforward equivalent of nonbarbarism." It is about the irreducibility of persons to Western categories of understanding, not about state surveillance — but it has ALREADY been imported into surveillance discourse (e.g., Zach Blas, "Informatic Opacity," per https://archive2.bakonline.org/prospections/informatic-opacity-by-zach-blas/, found in search). The draft's title-concept "democratic right to opacity" will read, to humanities-literate readers, as either homage or ignorance; one clause of acknowledgment converts a liability into depth.
