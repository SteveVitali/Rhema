# Schneidman (Protect Democracy), "DOGE's data 'panopticon' pales compared to what's next" (2025)

- **URL:** https://www.ifyoucankeepit.org/p/doges-data-panopticon-pales-compared
- **Accessed:** 2026-08-21
- **Author/date:** Nicole Schneidman, Protect Democracy's "If you can keep it," May 12, 2025.

**Establishes:** The pro-democracy-org version of the argument, one layer down: federal aggregation ("everything from Social Security to tax records to biometric data in a singular, AI-enabled hub") plus the coming fight over *state* datasets (DMV, student, voter, medical). Invokes post-Watergate protections and federalism as the structural defense. Shows the draft's "what must the state be prevented from knowing" question already live in the advocacy literature, framed as data-governance federalism rather than constitutional theory.
