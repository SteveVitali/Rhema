# Patel (Brennan Center), "Trump's Orders Targeting Anti-Fascism Aim to Criminalize Opposition" (2025)

- **URL:** https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/trumps-orders-targeting-antifascism-aim-criminalize-opposition
- **Accessed:** 2026-08-21
- **Author/date:** Faiza Patel, Oct. 9, 2025.

**Establishes:** The strongest legal-advocacy critique of the Antifa EO + NSPM-7: no factual predicate for an organized conspiracy; the ideological categories sweep in "labor organizers, socialists, many libertarians, those who criticize Christianity, pro-immigration groups, anti-ICE protestors, and racial justice and transgender activists"; concrete material-support hypotheticals ("Buying a sandwich for an activist... could all potentially be considered material support"); predicts court challenges "will likely meet with success." The draft cites Brennan only for ICE budget figures; this report is the closer neighbor to §V-§VI.
