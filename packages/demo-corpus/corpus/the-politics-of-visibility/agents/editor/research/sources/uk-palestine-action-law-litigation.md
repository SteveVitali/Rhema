Establishes: proscription dates; s.12(1A) "an opinion or belief that is supportive of a
proscribed organisation" + recklessness; s.13 "arouse reasonable suspicion that he is a
member or supporter". Litigation seesaw: High Court (Feb. 13, 2026) — unlawful,
disproportionate, only 3 of hundreds of activities met the terrorism definition and
"the ordinary criminal law was available to deal with those incidents"; CoA (June 15,
2026) reversed (covert operation, deference); UKSC permission granted July 2026, no
hearing as of 2026-08-21.
