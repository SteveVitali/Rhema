# Doctrine check: mosaic theory & practical obscurity

- **Accessed:** 2026-08-21

**Establishes:** Two existing terms of art the draft re-derives without naming. (1) Mosaic theory: aggregated data points, individually unprotected, become a Fourth Amendment "search" as a whole because the mosaic "reveals a great deal about a person's life, even if each piece... reveal[s] little in isolation" — this IS the draft's §IV combinatorial claim and §XV's "facts politically intelligible that were harmless apart." (2) "Practical obscurity" (Reporters Committee, 1989): the Supreme Court's own name for the privacy that inheres in information being hard to compile — the direct doctrinal ancestor of the draft's "practical opacity." Both are gifts: they give §XV constitutional pedigree, at the price of the novelty claim.
