# Chatrie v. United States (U.S., June 29, 2026)

- EFF: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/06/victory-supreme-court-says-constitution-protects-peoples-location-data
- The Conversation: https://theconversation.com/supreme-court-rules-your-cellphone-location-data-is-protected-by-the-fourth-amendment-286424
- Accessed 2026-08-21 (Phase 1).

Establishes: 6–3, June 29, 2026; obtaining app-generated cellphone location data from a
third party is a Fourth Amendment search; opinion quoted: "An individual has a legitimate
expectation of privacy in his cellphone location data."; extends Carpenter; geofence
warrant reasonableness/good-faith left unresolved, remanded to the Fourth Circuit.
