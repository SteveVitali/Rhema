## Structure norms binding this piece

- Roman-numeral `<h2>` sections with **declarative titles** (claims or noun
  phrases; never questions, never gerunds); an **unnumbered opening section**
  and an **unnumbered coda whose title echoes or completes the essay title**.
- **Epigraph**: one, attributed, naming the thinker whose frame the piece uses
  — contingent on verbatim verification (claim C48). All four 2026 pieces
  carry one.
- **Apparatus**: numbered endnote references `[n]` resolved in a References
  section with URLs; **zero footnotes**. Citation style: name the author and
  the claim in the sentence, carry bibliography in the note.
- **Counterarguments get structural positions**: a named steelman section, and
  an explicit limits move ("What This Does Not Solve" family).
- **Concrete cases are used once and dropped**; analogies run 2–5 sentences,
  never sustained as a conceit.
- **Enumerations are announced and then paid off.**
- **Ending**: 2–4 sentences, stated fact rather than judgment (judgment may be
  explicitly disclaimed); last sentence short (7–25 words) or one balanced
  periodic sentence returning the opening term.

## Anti-signature rations (the audit counts these)

- **Negation-then-correction is simultaneously the persona's signature and the
  machine's tic.** The ration (4–6/1k, forms varied) governs; monotony, not
  density, is the failure mode. Per `draft-from-outline` Phase 4: the recast-on-
  sight rule for "not X, but Y" is overridden by this ration.
- **Em dashes:** the model's strongest tell and this persona's most-watched
  number. Long-form band ≤1.4/1k. Convert to commas, colons, parentheses, or
  full stops.
- **Aphorism inflation**: this outline is aphorism-rich ("The machinery can
  precede the regime"; "the perfect chilling effect…"; "frictionless power…").
  Budget them — roughly one per section, placed at stress positions, never
  stacked.
- **Triads**: the subject invites list-of-three rhetoric (surveil, classify,
  punish). Hold to ≤4/1k, no stacking.
- **"This is why…"** ≤2 in the whole piece.
- **Rhetorical questions** only in structural positions; the outline's many
  interrogatives ("What exactly are we seeing?") mostly become declaratives.

## Register hazards (from `prose-standards.md` §5 + persona lexicon)

Marker vocabulary — defect at first appearance: delve, showcase, underscore,
intricate, notably, crucially, pivotal, robust, nuanced, multifaceted, tapestry,
landscape/realm/navigate (as metaphor), testament, leverage (verb), foster,
myriad, plethora, ever-evolving, in today's world. Also: hedge stacks
(scope limits stated flatly instead), throat-clearing openings, summary
paragraphs restating the paragraph above, symmetrical both-sidesing ("the truth
is somewhere in between" — this essay has a position), "not only… but also" as
filler, stacked tricolons. **No both-sides throat-clearing**: the corpus states
its commitments; hedges appear only as stated scope limits ("this cannot show
what a naive reading would want it to show").
