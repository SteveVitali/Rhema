# Structure plan — The Politics of Visibility, v1

Fidelity: `adaptive`. The outline's 26 sections + opening + appendix are
consolidated into an unnumbered opening, **15 roman-numeral sections**, an
unnumbered coda, and a compact appendix — the persona's long-form grammar
(model piece: 14 sections at 6,700 words). Reading order justified against the
outline's order in **Deviations** below.

## The spine (each step a claim)

1. The Minnesota investigation is neither ordinary policing nor totalitarianism;
   the vocabulary we reach for fails it, and the failure is the subject.
   *(Opening)*
2. Democracies now lose liberal substance through law rather than against it,
   so "was it legal?" is the wrong test. *(I)*
3. To govern is first to see: state capacity begins in legibility, and digital
   legibility has shifted from identity to behavior — from who you are to whom
   you appear beside. *(II)*
4. No single eye watches. A modular, largely private ecosystem observes, and
   its political danger is interoperability, not any one camera. *(III)*
5. Fusion converts observations into relationships; capacity grows
   combinatorially, and the endpoint (spyware) closes the last gap — while
   constitutional doctrine adapts behind the technology, not never. *(IV)*
6. Seeing becomes targeting only through classification, and current doctrine
   (NSPM-7) supplies ideological classifiers that turn protected belief into
   investigative predicate. *(V)*
7. "Terrorism" is the word that converts classification into legal exception —
   America still lacks the domestic-proscription statute that completes the
   conversion, and Britain shows precisely what having it means. *(VI)*
8. The right model is Fraenkel's dual state, not the police state: zones of
   thinned legal protection already exist, and immigration is the laboratory.
   *(VII)*
9. None of this is foreign to America — Palmer, COINTELPRO, Muslim
   surveillance — and the capacity is bipartisan and inherited. *(VIII)*
10. What is new is not political surveillance but its economics: scale,
    persistence, integration, automation — surveillance that once required
    armies of agents becomes a query. *(IX)*
11. The machinery's most efficient product is not arrest but the quiet
    contraction of association — and association is the generative mechanism of
    democratic power, so the liberal question becomes what a citizen must
    reasonably fear. *(X, XI)*
12. America is not the dictatorship this machinery could serve; the honest
    diagnosis is a set of falsifiable thresholds, and the checks still bind —
    which is the reason to defend them, not to relax. *(XII)*
13. Therefore some opacity between citizen and state is democratic
    infrastructure, not a consumer preference — and its defense is a civic
    practice with principles, not an app. *(XIII, XIV)*
14. The constitutional question of the surveillance age is what power must be
    structurally prevented from knowing: the technological ideal is
    frictionless power; the constitutional ideal is power subjected to
    friction. *(XV)*
15. The checks matter until the day they do not; the moment to defend them is
    while they still do. *(Coda)*

## The opening, concretely

- **Move**: documented miniature (persona opening variant 5, long-form
  attested) — the Minnesota record rendered in three or four factual sentences,
  cited: undercover officers inside civic meetings; plate readers outside;
  administrative summonses for years of financial records; a federal diagram
  titled "The Conspiracy" holding eighteen organizations, none of them charged.
  No second person, no scene-dressing, no invented detail; every sentence
  traceable to C1–C3.
- **Then the turn**: concede the intuition ("this sounds totalitarian"),
  correct it with the evidence against it (defense lawyers obtained the
  documents; journalists published them; courts scrutinize them), and diagnose:
  the coexistence is the object. Negation-then-correction lands here.
- **The promise**: the essay will say what we are actually seeing, how much of
  the machinery already exists, and what would have to change before the
  diagnosis changes.
- **Stakes clear by**: end of paragraph two.
- **Epigraph** above it (pending C48 verification): Fraenkel preferred, Shklar
  acceptable; none if nothing verifies verbatim.

## The ending, concretely

The coda pays the opening's promise by returning to the meeting at the moment
before it happens: the citizen who stays home. Then the final movement, close
to the outline's own formulation (a commitment): the question is not whether
the machinery has been turned, but whether a free society should wait until it
is before deciding that its construction mattered — closing on the outline's
last line about spaces in which citizens need not imagine themselves through
the eyes of the state. Ending register: stated fact; last sentence either
short (≤25 words) or one balanced periodic sentence returning "visibility"/
"seen" — completing the title.

## Deviations from the outline's structure (fidelity: adaptive)

1. **Chatrie moved** from the panopticon section (outline III.D) into IV — the
   constitutional-lag point lands harder after fusion is established, and IV
   needs its note of institutional resistance so III does not end in fatalism.
2. **Britain merged into the terrorism section** (outline V+VI → my VI) — the
   UK case is the payoff of the missing-statute argument, not a separate
   comparison; separation forced the reader to hold the statute point across a
   section boundary.
3. **Fraenkel and immigration merged** (outline VII+VIII → my VII) — the dual
   state without its laboratory is 450 words of exposition; the laboratory
   without the model is anecdote. Together they are one argument.
4. **"What is actually new" moved before the chilling material** (outline XV →
   my IX, directly after history) — it answers the objection history raises at
   the moment the reader raises it; the outline's placement (after Foucault)
   makes the steelman arrive late.
5. **"What This Is Not" and the thresholds merged with "Why the Checks Still
   Matter"** (outline XVI+XVII+XXV → my XII, with the checks material also
   feeding the coda) — all three are the same calibration move; XXV's content
   additionally seeds the coda's non-fatalist ending.
6. **The paradox, the design principles, friction, and "what must power not
   know" merged** (outline XXII+XXIII+XXIV → my XV) — they are one argument
   climbing one hill; the outline's separation is research-file structure.
7. **Hungary relocated** from the comparative ladder to XII — it is evidence of
   contingency, which is XII's function.
8. **Tocqueville/Arendt and Shklar merged** (outline XI+XII → my XI) — Shklar's
   fear argument completes the association argument; the outline's "relatively
   late" instruction for Shklar is honored within the section.
9. **Section count 15 + opening + coda** vs. persona's typical 10–14: one over
   the observed band, proportionate to the corpus-maximum length; accepted and
   logged.
