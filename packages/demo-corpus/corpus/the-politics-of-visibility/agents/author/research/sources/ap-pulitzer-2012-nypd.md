- **What it establishes:** AP's Matt Apuzzo, Adam Goldman, Eileen Sullivan, and Chris Hawley won the 2012 Pulitzer Prize for Investigative Reporting for the NYPD Muslim-surveillance series (begun August 2011). The Pulitzer board cited their "spotlighting of the New York Police Department's clandestine spying program" that monitored daily life in Muslim communities. The series documented that the NYPD, with CIA assistance, built surveillance of Muslim neighborhoods, businesses, student groups, and mosques — including infiltration, photographing individuals, and tracking license plates — and prompted congressional calls for a federal investigation.
