# eff-ssd-mobile-location-tracking

- **URL:** https://ssd.eff.org/module/mobile-phones-location-tracking · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claims C5–C13
- **What it establishes:** EFF Surveillance Self-Defense module "Mobile Phones: Location Tracking" (last updated Nov 5, 2024) identifies four distinct location-exposure vectors, each with its own section heading: "Mobile Signal Tracking — Towers"; "Mobile Signal Tracking — Cell Site Simulator"; "Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Tracking"; "Location Information Leaks From Apps and Web Browsing."
- **Key passages:** Towers: "There is no way to hide from this kind of tracking as long as your mobile phone is powered on, with a registered SIM card, and transmitting signals." Wi-Fi/Bluetooth: devices broadcast MAC addresses in probe requests; "These identifiers have traditionally been valuable tools for passive trackers in retail stores and coffee shops to gather data." Apps: "location tracking could be used to suggest when people are in a romantic relationship by following who they visit."
- **Reliability:** Secondary/advocacy but technically authoritative; EFF's canonical security-education resource.
- **Relevance to this project:** C10(b) (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi as tracking/fingerprinting vectors) and C10(c) (cellular vs. Wi-Fi/Bluetooth vs. apps as distinct exposure channels).
