- **What it establishes:** Sept. 30, 2025: Judge William G. Young (D. Mass., Reagan appointee), after a bench trial in AAUP v. Rubio (1:25-cv-10685), found that Rubio, Noem, and subordinates unconstitutionally targeted noncitizen pro-Palestinian advocates for deportation to suppress protected political speech — the "ideological deportation" policy of which Khalil's arrest was a leading instance. 161-page opinion; remedies set for later hearings.

"This is a new invention that in important ways goes beyond its closest analogues in the Red Scare." "ICE goes masked for a single reason — to terrorize Americans into quiescence."
