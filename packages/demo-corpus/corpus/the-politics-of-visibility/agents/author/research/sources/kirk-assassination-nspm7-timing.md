# kirk-assassination-nspm7-timing
- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Charlie_Kirk ; https://ballotpedia.org/Executive_Order:_Designating_Antifa_as_a_Domestic_Terrorist_Organization_(Donald_Trump,_2025) ; https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/09/countering-domestic-terrorism-and-organized-political-violence/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSPM-7 · **Accessed:** 2026-08-25 · **For:** v1→v2 response round, verifying the NSPM-7 timing claim
- **What it establishes:** The three dates and the intervals, each multiply sourced:
  - **Sept. 10, 2025** — Charlie Kirk (31, founder of Turning Point USA) assassinated at Utah Valley University, Orem, Utah, at 12:23 p.m. MDT, while debating students at an outdoor event; sniper shot; suspect Tyler Robinson surrendered Sept. 11. (Wikipedia; UVU's own news page; FBI press page "Utah Valley Shooting Updates"; ABC News timeline.)
  - **Sept. 22, 2025** — Trump signs the executive order "Designating Antifa as a Domestic Terrorist Organization." (Ballotpedia; White House fact sheet via American Presidency Project; Brennan Center analysis, which notes the order "issued on September 22, 2025, has no legal basis and does not cite one.")
  - **Sept. 25, 2025** — NSPM-7, "Countering Domestic Terrorism and Organized Political Violence," issued. (whitehouse.gov presidential-actions page dated 2025/09; globalsecurity.org mirror filename dated 20250925; Wikipedia NSPM-7 article; DLA Piper and Morgan Lewis client alerts.)
  - **Intervals (arithmetic checked): NSPM-7 came fifteen days after the assassination (Sept. 10 → Sept. 25) and three days after the Antifa EO (Sept. 22 → Sept. 25).** The EO came twelve days after the assassination.
- **Key passages:**
  - Antifa EO characterizes antifa as "a militarist, anarchist enterprise that calls for the overthrow of the U.S. government, law enforcement authorities, and our system of law—using illegal means, including violence and terrorism, to accomplish these goals" and directs government to "investigate, disrupt, and dismantle" its operations and funding. (Via EO text as quoted in ICCT/Ballotpedia coverage.)
  - NSPM-7 tasks agencies to "investigate, prosecute, and disrupt entities and individuals engaged in acts of political violence and intimidation" and to "disrupt financial networks that fund domestic terrorism and political violence"; the accompanying White House fact sheet says the "approach will focus on all aspects of the organized strategy that results in political violence and intimidation, including radicalization, recruitment, funding mechanisms, non-governmental organization involvement..." (search-level for exact punctuation; verify against whitehouse.gov text before print quotation).
  - Follow-on: AG Bondi's implementing memorandum to all federal prosecutors, Dec. 4, 2025 (Charity & Security Network; ACLU analysis).
- **Reliability:** Dates are primary-source anchored (whitehouse.gov, FBI, UVU) with heavy independent corroboration across the political spectrum (Fox, ABC, Brennan Center, Cato, law-firm alerts). High confidence on all three dates.
- **Relevance to this project:** Verifies the essay's timing sentence precisely: NSPM-7 issued fifteen days after Kirk's assassination and three days after the Antifa designation EO — the escalation sequence the essay uses to frame the 2025–26 domestic-surveillance turn (and the predicate Democracy Now cites for Operation Puppet Master; see democracynow-nspm7-link-assertion).
