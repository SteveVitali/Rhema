# flock-faq-scale-capture

- **URL:** https://www.flocksafety.com/faq · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claims C5–C13
- **What it establishes:** Flock's own (first-party) account of what its LPR cameras capture and its scale claims: "currently in 5,000+ communities"; cameras capture license plate number, make and model, color, resident vs. non-resident status, timestamp, plate type (standard vs. temporary), "damage or alterations (i.e. broken taillight, after-market wheels)," body type, and distinguishing features like "roof racks, back racks, aftermarket wheels." Default retention: "Data from Flock Safety devices is stored and then deleted after 7 days" (reflects the 2026 policy change; was 30 days). A "National LPR Network" product exists; hot-list alerts integrate with NCIC.
- **Key passages:** "Damage or alterations (i.e. broken taillight, after-market wheels)"; "Data from Flock Safety devices is stored and then deleted after 7 days"; "The neighborhood, business, or community determines who has access to footage."
- **Reliability:** Primary (company self-description); promotional bias, use for capability claims and company-stated figures, not for independent scale verification.
- **Relevance to this project:** C5 (what the cameras record; searchability; retention). Cross-check scale against Stateline reporting (stateline-flock-2026-scale).
