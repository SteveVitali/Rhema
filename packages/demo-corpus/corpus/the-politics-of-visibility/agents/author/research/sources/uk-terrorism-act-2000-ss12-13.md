# uk-terrorism-act-2000-ss12-13
- **URL:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/12 and https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/11/section/13 · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claims C17–C19
- **What it establishes:** The consequences of proscription the essay invokes. s.11 (not separately fetched; offence of membership is standard and reflected in the SI's explanatory materials and ProtectUK guidance) — membership offence. s.12: offence to invite support for a proscribed organisation (support beyond money/property), and — s.12(1A), added 2019 — to express "an opinion or belief that is supportive of a proscribed organisation" while reckless as to whether a listener will be encouraged to support it; also meeting-related offences. Penalty on indictment: up to 14 years (raised 2021). s.13: summary offence to wear clothing or carry or display an article "in such a way or in such circumstances as to arouse reasonable suspicion that he is a member or supporter of a proscribed organisation" (s.13(1)); s.13(1A) extends to publishing images; police may seize items; penalty up to 6 months / level 5 fine.
- **Key passages:** s.13(1) "reasonable suspicion that he is a member or supporter of a proscribed organisation" standard — this is the provision under which sign-holders were arrested/charged; Home Office data (year ending Dec. 2025) shows 404 Palestine Action-linked charges under s.13 vs 8 under s.12.
- **Reliability:** Primary — official consolidated statute text on legislation.gov.uk.
- **Relevance to this project:** C17 (what proscription criminalises), C18–C19 (statutory basis of sign arrests). Precision note: signs are s.13 (display of articles, summary offence, 6 months max); spoken/reckless expressions of support are s.12(1A) (up to 14 years). Don't conflate.
