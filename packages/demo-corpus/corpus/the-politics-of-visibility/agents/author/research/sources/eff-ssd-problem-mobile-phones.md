# eff-ssd-problem-mobile-phones
- **URL:** https://ssd.eff.org/module/problem-mobile-phones · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C43
- **What it establishes:** EFF's SSD identifies distinct location-exposure channels: (1) cellular network signal tracking, (2) Wi-Fi/Bluetooth MAC-address tracking, (3) app/GPS location leaks — plus cell-site simulators as a separate direct-collection technique.
- **Key passages:**
  - Cellular: "The operator can calculate where a particular subscriber's phone is located whenever the phone is powered on and registered with the network."
  - Wi-Fi/Bluetooth: "Both of these kinds of wireless signals include a unique serial number for the device, called a MAC address, which can be seen by anybody who can receive the signal."
  - Apps/GPS: "Some of these apps will then transmit your location over the network to a service provider, which, in turn, provides a way for other people to track you."
- **Reliability:** Primary for EFF's guidance.
- **Relevance to this project:** C43(b) — the distinct-channels claim.
