# home-office-tact-stats-dec-2025
- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/operation-of-police-powers-under-tact-2000-to-december-2025/operation-of-police-powers-under-the-terrorism-act-2000-and-subsequent-legislation-arrests-outcomes-and-stop-and-search-great-britain-quarterly-u · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C18 (also C19c)
- **What it establishes:** Home Office accredited official statistics, "Operation of police powers under the Terrorism Act 2000 … quarterly update to December 2025" (published 12 March 2026). This — not an annual report and accounts — is the correct source for the arrest figures, and it confirms the claim's numbers exactly.
- **Key passages:** "there were 3,034 arrests for terrorism-related activity in the year ending 31 December 2025" — a rise of 1,114% on the previous year (250). · "There were 2,779 arrests for terrorism-related activity in the year ending 31 December 2025 linked to supporting Palestine Action, following its proscription on 5 July 2025." (2,779/3,034 = 92%.) · Of Palestine Action-linked arrests, "412 were charged, including 8 under Section 12 and 404 under Section 13 of the Terrorism Act 2000." · Definition: arrests in Great Britain "where there was suspicion of involvement in terrorist-related activity, either at the time of arrest or later in the investigation."
- **Reliability:** Primary — official government statistical release (accredited official statistics), Great Britain coverage.
