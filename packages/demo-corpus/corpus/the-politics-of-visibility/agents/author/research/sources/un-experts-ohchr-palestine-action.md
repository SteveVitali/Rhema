# un-experts-ohchr-palestine-action
- **URL:** https://www.ohchr.org/en/press-releases/2025/07/un-experts-urge-united-kingdom-not-misuse-terrorism-laws-against-protest (UN experts, 1 July 2025); https://www.ohchr.org/en/press-releases/2025/07/uk-palestine-action-ban-disturbing-misuse-uk-counter-terrorism-legislation (High Commissioner Türk, July 2025) · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 (see caveat) · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C19(b)
- **What it establishes:** (1) 1 July 2025: five UN special-procedures experts — Ben Saul (SR counter-terrorism and human rights), George Katrougalos, Irene Khan, Gina Romero, Francesca Albanese — urged the UK not to misuse terrorism laws against Palestine Action. (2) July 2025: High Commissioner Volker Türk called the ban a "disturbing" misuse of UK counter-terrorism legislation. (3) February 2026: a UN expert welcomed the High Court finding the proscription unlawful (https://www.ohchr.org/en/statements/2026/02/un-expert-welcomes-united-kingdom-court-finding-proscription-palestine-action).
- **Key passages (as excerpted in search results and consistent secondary coverage):** Experts: "[M]ere property damage, without endangering life, is not sufficiently serious to qualify as terrorism" and "[p]rotest actions that are not genuinely 'terrorist', but which involve alleged property damage, should be properly investigated as ordinary crimes or other security offences." Türk: the decision "raises serious concerns that counter-terrorism laws are being applied to conduct that is not terrorist in nature and risks hindering the legitimate exercise of fundamental freedoms."
