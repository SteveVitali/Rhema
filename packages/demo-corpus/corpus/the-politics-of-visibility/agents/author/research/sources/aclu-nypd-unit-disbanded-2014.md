# aclu-nypd-unit-disbanded-2014
- **URL:** https://www.aclu.org/press-releases/nypd-shuts-unit-mapped-muslim-communities · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C31
- **What it establishes:** In April 2014 the NYPD disbanded the Demographics Unit (by then renamed the Zone Assessment Unit). Contemporaneous coverage (CNN, Brennan Center, NYC mayor's office statement, per 2026-08-20 search) dates the announcement April 15, 2014, notes the unit was created ca. 2003 with CIA help, and that it had been largely inactive under Commissioner Bratton.
- **Key passages:**
  - "The NYPD's disbanding of a unit that targeted New York Muslims and mapped their everyday institutions and activities is a welcome first step."
  - "The Demographics Unit was only one component of a huge, discriminatory surveillance program that has sent informants and NYPD officers to spy on mosques, charities, student groups, and other mainstays of New York Muslim life."
- **Reliability:** Secondary (advocacy press release reacting to NYPD's announcement); disbanding itself is uncontested and multiply reported.
- **Relevance to this project:** C31 — "disbanded 2014" element; also the caution that disbanding one unit did not end the broader program.
