# aclu-galati-testimony-2012
- **URL:** https://www.aclu.org/press-releases/nypd-testimony-muslim-surveillance-operation-highlights-futility-spying-innocent · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C31
- **What it establishes:** NYPD Assistant Chief Thomas Galati (commanding officer, Intelligence Division), in a June 28, 2012 deposition unsealed in the *Handschu v. Special Services Division* litigation, testified under oath that the Demographics Unit's work never generated a lead.
- **Key passages:**
  - Galati, verbatim: "I never made a lead from rhetoric that came from a Demographics report, and I'm here since 2006." He added, "I don't recall other ones prior to my arrival."
  - Context: the deposition was discovery in the decades-old Handschu federal case, under a 1985 consent decree restricting NYPD surveillance of political/religious activity absent "specific information" linking a group to crime.
- **Reliability:** Secondary (advocacy press release), but it quotes sworn testimony verbatim and matches AP's contemporaneous reporting (AP, Aug. 21, 2012, Apuzzo/Goldman: "NYPD: Muslim spying led to no leads, terror cases"). ACLU was counsel-adjacent in related litigation — treat characterizations as adversarial, the quote itself as record.
- **Relevance to this project:** C31 — the load-bearing "no leads by the unit chief's sworn testimony" specific.
