# uk-si-2025-803-proscription-order
- **URL:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/803/made · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claims C17, C19
- **What it establishes:** The Terrorism Act 2000 (Proscribed Organisations) (Amendment) Order 2025, SI 2025/803 — made 4 July 2025, in force 5 July 2025. Article 2 adds three organisations to Schedule 2 of the Terrorism Act 2000: Palestine Action; Maniacs Murder Cult (MMC) (MKU) (MKY); Russian Imperial Movement (RIM) (RID), including Russian Imperial Legion (RIL). A note specifies the Palestine Action entry means "the organisation using that name that is based in the United Kingdom."
- **Key passages:** Made "4th July 2025"; coming into force "5th July 2025"; operative text inserts the entries into Schedule 2 after "Terrorgram collective."
- **Reliability:** Primary — the statutory instrument itself, official legislation.gov.uk text.
- **Relevance to this project:** C17 (proscription date/instrument). Timeline corroborated elsewhere: Commons approved 385–26 on 2 July 2025, Lords 3 July 2025, in force 00:01 on 5 July 2025.
