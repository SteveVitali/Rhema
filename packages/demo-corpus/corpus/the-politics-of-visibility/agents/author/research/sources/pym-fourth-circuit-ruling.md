# pym-fourth-circuit-ruling
- **URL:** https://www.pym.org/appeals-court-backs-philadelphia-yearly-meeting-in-lawsuit-against-trump-administration-press-release/ · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C46
- **What it establishes:** Aug. 18, 2026: the Fourth Circuit (No. 25-1512, published; Justia lists it as Yearly Meeting of the Religious Society of Friends v. DHS) AFFIRMED Judge Theodore Chuang's Feb. 24, 2025 preliminary injunction (D. Md., 8:25-cv-00243) barring warrantless immigration-enforcement actions "in or near the places of worship owned, operated, occupied, or used by" the plaintiffs — Quaker meetings (Philadelphia, New York, New England, Baltimore Yearly Meetings), Cooperative Baptist Fellowship, and the Sikh Temple of Sacramento. District court found plaintiffs likely to succeed on their RFRA/expressive-association challenge to the Jan. 2025 rescission of the sensitive-locations policy. Injunction is plaintiff-specific, NOT nationwide; litigation continues.
- **Key passages:** PYM General Secretary Chris Mohr: "We are relieved that our meetinghouses continue to be safe for all community members to gather for worship while the case moves through the legal process."
- **Reliability:** Party press release (advocacy) — but the ruling's existence, date, and case number are corroborated by Justia (25-1512, decided 2026-08-18), Democracy Forward, Courthouse News, and the CA4 published-opinion PDF (ca4.uscourts.gov/opinions/251512.P.pdf, not machine-readable this session).
- **Relevance to this project:** C46 — this IS the citable ruling: a court has constrained immigration enforcement at (plaintiff) houses of worship, affirmed on appeal two days before the essay's date. Scope must be stated as plaintiff-specific and preliminary.
