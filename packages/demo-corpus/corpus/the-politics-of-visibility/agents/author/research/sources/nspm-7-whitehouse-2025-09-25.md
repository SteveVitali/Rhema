# nspm-7-whitehouse-2025-09-25
- **URL:** https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/09/countering-domestic-terrorism-and-organized-political-violence/ · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 (full text retrieved and read) · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C4
- **What it establishes:** Primary text of NSPM-7. Formal designation: "NATIONAL SECURITY PRESIDENTIAL MEMORANDUM/NSPM-7"; subject: "Countering Domestic Terrorism and Organized Political Violence"; dated September 25, 2025; addressed to the Secretaries of State, Treasury, Homeland Security and the Attorney General; signed Donald J. Trump. Confirms all three C4 sub-claims verbatim.
- **Key passages:** (a) Structures/networks/funding/predicate conduct — Sec. 1: "A new law enforcement strategy that investigates all participants in these criminal and terroristic conspiracies — including the organized structures, networks, entities, organizations, funding sources, and predicate actions behind them — is required." Also Sec. 1: "The United States requires a national strategy to investigate and disrupt networks, entities, and organizations that foment political violence so that law enforcement can intervene in criminal conspiracies before they result in violent political acts... law enforcement will disband and uproot networks, entities, and organizations that promote organized violence, violent intimidation, conspiracies against rights, and other efforts to disrupt the functioning of a democratic society."
  (b) Ideological indicators — Sec. 1: "Common threads animating this violent conduct include anti-Americanism, anti-capitalism, and anti-Christianity; support for the overthrow of the United States Government; extremism on migration, race, and gender; and hostility towards those who hold traditional American views on family, religion, and morality." (Note: the memo's exact terms are "extremism on migration, race, and gender" and "hostility towards those who hold traditional American views on family, religion, and morality" — not "traditional conceptions of family.")
  (c) Agency directives — Sec. 2(a): JTTFs "shall coordinate and supervise a comprehensive national strategy to investigate, prosecute, and disrupt entities and individuals engaged in acts of political violence and intimidation designed to suppress lawful political activity or obstruct the rule of law." Sec. 2(c)(i): JTTFs shall investigate "institutional and individual funders, and officers and employees of organizations, that are responsible for, sponsor, or otherwise aid and abet the principal actors engaging in the criminal conduct." Sec. 2(g): the AG shall direct DOJ "to prosecute all Federal crimes, to the maximum extent permissible by law, related to the investigations described..." Sec. 2(i): Treasury "shall make available all resources, to the maximum extent permitted by law, to identify and disrupt financial networks that fund domestic terrorism and political violence," acting through the Terrorism and Financial Intelligence Office, incl. SAR guidance to banks. Sec. 2(j): the IRS Commissioner "shall take action to ensure that no tax-exempt entities are directly or indirectly financing political violence or domestic terrorism," with referrals to DOJ. Sec. 2(k): investigations "should prioritize crimes such as": 18 U.S.C. 111, 241, 371, 373, 1956, 2339/2339A–D, 844, RICO (1961 et seq.), 1031. Sec. 3: AG may recommend groups for designation as a "domestic terrorist organization." Sec. 4: AG and DHS Secretary "shall designate domestic terrorism a national priority area and develop appropriate grant programs." Cross-references the Sept. 22, 2025 order "Designating Antifa as a Domestic Terrorist Organization."
- **Reliability:** Primary (official White House publication of the memorandum; Sec. 5(d) directs DHS to publish it in the Federal Register).
