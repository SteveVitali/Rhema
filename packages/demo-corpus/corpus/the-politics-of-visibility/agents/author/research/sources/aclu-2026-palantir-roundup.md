# aclu-2026-palantir-roundup

- **URL:** https://www.aclu.org/news/privacy-technology/palantir-deportation-roundup · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claims C5–C13
- **What it establishes:** ACLU, "All the Ways Palantir is Assisting Trump's Abusive Removal Campaign" (Sophie Feng & Jay Stanley, March 24, 2026). Documents the Palantir/ICE product stack: Investigative Case Management (ICM) — Palantir-built since 2014, sole-source deal ballooned to over $145 million, "backbone" of enforcement, spanning case lifecycle; FALCON (used since 2012, possibly superseded); ImmigrationOS — $30M contract entered April 2025 (prototype due Sept 25, 2025, runs through Sept 2027), AI augmentation of ICM for apprehension ops, monitoring "self-deportations" and visa overstays with "near real-time visibility"; ELITE (Enhanced Leads Identification and Targeting for Enforcement) — deportation-target prioritization with map interface and "address confidence scores"; AI tip processing. Data unified: schooling, family relationships, employment, phone records, immigration history, personal connections, biometric traits, criminal records, addresses; feeds from HHS, USCIS, FBI, DEA, ATF, SEVIS, and commercial brokers (Thomson Reuters CLEAR).
- **Key passages:** "U.S. Citizens are still subject to criminal prosecution and thus are a part of ICM."
- **Reliability:** Secondary/advocacy, but heavily footnoted to 404 Media, The Intercept, WIRED, DHS Privacy Impact Assessments, EPIC litigation, and sam.gov contract records.
- **Relevance to this project:** C11 — supplies the citable basis the outline lacked: case records, identity/immigration records, addresses, relationships, investigative leads, and geospatial/map targeting are all documented; continuous "movement" tracking is the one element to phrase carefully (location/address intelligence and map-based targeting, not a proven live movement feed).
