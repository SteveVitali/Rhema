# brennan-deportation-industrial-complex
- **URL:** https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/analysis-opinion/big-budget-act-creates-deportation-industrial-complex · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C42
- **What it establishes:** Analysis (Margy O'Herron, Aug. 13, 2025) of the 2025 reconciliation act (H.R. 1, signed July 4, 2025): "more than $170 billion over four years" for immigration enforcement overall; ICE receives "$75 billion over four years, approximately $18.7 billion each year"; of that, "$45 billion over four years" is for detention ("two-thirds of ICE's funding"); "The $11.25 billion added to ICE's annual detention budget is a 400% increase from last year"; combined with the "$10 billion Congress already appropriated ICE for fiscal year 2025," ICE had "$28.7 billion at its disposal this year... nearly triple ICE's entire budget for FY24"; funds 10,000 new ICE officers; detention capacity "potentially more than 100,000 people per year."
