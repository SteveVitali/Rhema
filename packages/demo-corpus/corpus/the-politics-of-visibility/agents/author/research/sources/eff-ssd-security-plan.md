# eff-ssd-security-plan
- **URL:** https://ssd.eff.org/module/your-security-plan · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C43
- **What it establishes:** EFF Surveillance Self-Defense frames security around individual security plans/threat models, not universal configurations.
- **Key passages:**
  - "Security isn't just about the tools you use or the software you download. It begins with understanding the unique threats you face and how you can counter those threats."
  - "there is no perfect option for security" ... "your risk assessment will allow you to plan the right strategy for you, balancing convenience, cost, and privacy."
  - Core questions: "What do I want to protect?"; "Who do I want to protect it from?"; "How bad are the consequences if I fail?"; "How likely is it that I will need to protect it?"; "How much trouble am I willing to go through to try to prevent potential consequences?"; "Who are my allies?"
  - "assessing risks is both a personal and a subjective process."
- **Reliability:** Primary for what EFF's guidance says (EFF's own resource).
- **Relevance to this project:** C43(a) — threat-model framing.
