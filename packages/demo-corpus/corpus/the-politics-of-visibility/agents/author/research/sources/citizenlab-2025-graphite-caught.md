# citizenlab-2025-graphite-caught

- **URL:** https://citizenlab.ca/research/first-forensic-confirmation-of-paragons-ios-mercenary-spyware-finds-journalists-targeted/ · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claims C5–C13
- **What it establishes:** Citizen Lab, "Graphite Caught: First Forensic Confirmation of Paragon's iOS Mercenary Spyware Finds Journalists Targeted" (Bill Marczak & John Scott-Railton, June 12, 2025). Forensic confirmation that Italian journalist Ciro Pellegrino (head of Fanpage.it's Naples newsroom) and a prominent European journalist (anonymous) were targeted with Paragon's Graphite via an iMessage zero-click attack (CVE-2025-43200, mitigated in iOS 18.3.1); both devices communicated with a server attributed to the same Paragon operator. Prior targets in the Paragon cluster: Fanpage.it editor Francesco Cancellato, Mediterranea Saving Humans' Luca Casarini and Giuseppe "Beppe" Caccia (Android cases; the Italian government acknowledged using Paragon against Casarini and Caccia). Builds on Citizen Lab's March 2025 report "A First Look at Paragon's Proliferating Spyware Operations."
- **Key passages:** "Our analysis finds forensic evidence confirming with high confidence that both a prominent European journalist...and Italian journalist Ciro Pellegrino, were targeted with Paragon's Graphite mercenary spyware." "We identify an indicator linking both cases to the same Paragon operator."
- **Reliability:** Primary forensic research; Citizen Lab (U. Toronto) is the field's authoritative lab; Apple corroborated the exploit fix.
- **Relevance to this project:** C13 (Paragon Graphite used against journalists/activists internationally, with named cases) and context for C12 (what Graphite is and does).
