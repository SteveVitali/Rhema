# gao-23-105607-facial-recognition

- **URL:** https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105607 · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claims C5–C13
- **What it establishes:** GAO, "Facial Recognition Services: Federal Law Enforcement Agencies Should Take Actions to Implement Training, and Policies for Civil Liberties" (GAO-23-105607, Sept. 5, 2023; released Sept. 12, 2023). Seven federal law enforcement agencies used commercial facial recognition services Oct 2019–Mar 2022: FBI, ICE-HSI, CBP, U.S. Secret Service, DEA, ATF, U.S. Marshals Service. Clearview AI explicitly among the four services. "Six agencies had available data and cumulatively conducted about 60,000 searches when they did not have training requirements in place." Only three of seven agencies had civil-rights/civil-liberties policies specific to the technology.
- **Key passages:** As quoted above.
- **Reliability:** Primary (government audit body); authoritative and nonpartisan.
- **Relevance to this project:** C9 (operational facial-recognition search capability at CBP, FBI, HSI et al. — demonstrated use, not just capability: ~60,000 actual searches).
