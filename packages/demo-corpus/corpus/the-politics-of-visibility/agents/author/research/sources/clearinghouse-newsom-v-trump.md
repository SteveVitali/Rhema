# clearinghouse-newsom-v-trump
- **URL:** https://clearinghouse.net/case/46678/ · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C27
- **What it establishes:** Newsom v. Trump, 3:25-cv-04870 (N.D. Cal., Judge Charles Breyer). June 2025: Secretary Hegseth federalized 2,000 CA National Guard (June 9) + 2,000 more (June 10) without the Governor's consent — 4,000 total. Sept. 2, 2025: Breyer held "federalized National Guard units engaged in prohibited law enforcement activities in Los Angeles pursuant to a top-down directive and that § 12406 did not create an exception to the Act" — a Posse Comitatus Act violation; injunction against further use of the Guard to execute domestic law, stayed to Sept. 12 for appeal. Ninth Circuit stayed portions; Dec. 10, 2025 renewed preliminary injunction; Dec. 31, 2025 the Ninth Circuit vacated its administrative stay in full, leaving the Dec. 10 injunction fully operative. After SCOTUS's Trump v. Illinois (Dec. 23, 2025), the government withdrew its stay motion and on Jan. 23, 2026 moved to voluntarily dismiss its appeals (25-3727, 25-7781); plaintiffs opposed dismissal, wanting the prior stay order vacated. District-court briefing stayed pending appellate guidance; case ongoing.
- **Key passages:** As above (quoted holding); injunction "enjoined further use of the National Guard to execute domestic law."
- **Reliability:** Secondary but docket-grounded (Civil Rights Litigation Clearinghouse). Note: this page covers the Guard; the ~700 Marines figure is separately sourced (Stars and Stripes).
- **Relevance to this project:** C27 — ruling scope (PCA, statutory — not a constitutional holding), and honest appellate posture: never reversed on the merits; government abandoned its appeals after Trump v. Illinois.
