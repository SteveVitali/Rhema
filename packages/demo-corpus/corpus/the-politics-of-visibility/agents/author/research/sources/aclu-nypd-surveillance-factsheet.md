# aclu-nypd-surveillance-factsheet
- **URL:** https://www.aclu.org/other/factsheet-nypd-muslim-surveillance-program · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C31
- **What it establishes:** Program mechanics: community/business mapping by the Demographics Unit, mosque photo/video surveillance, license-plate logging at mosques, "mosque crawler" informants.
- **Key passages:**
  - "The NYPD's Demographics Unit (now the Zone Assessment Unit) has mapped neighborhoods predominantly occupied by 28 so-called 'ancestries of interest'"
  - "NYPD officers, stationed in cars outside of mosques, have taken pictures and video of those leaving and entering places of worship" and "recorded the license plate numbers of worshippers attending services"
  - "The NYPD has recruited so-called 'mosque crawlers,' to act as inside observers in mosques. They report on sermons, provide names of attendees"
- **Reliability:** Secondary (advocacy factsheet) but drawn from the AP document trove and litigation record; consistent with AP's Pulitzer-cited series.
- **Relevance to this project:** C31 — mapping, mosque monitoring, informants, license-plate specifics.
