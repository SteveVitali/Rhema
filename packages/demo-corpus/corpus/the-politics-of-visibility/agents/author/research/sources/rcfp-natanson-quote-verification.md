- **What it establishes:** The full quotation from Magistrate Judge William B. Porter's ruling (E.D. Va., decision issued Feb. 24, 2026; reported Feb. 25) in the Hannah Natanson device-search matter ends "...the Washington Post's henhouse," NOT the generic "...the henhouse." An essay rendering of "the equivalent of leaving the government's fox in charge of the henhouse" is a truncation; the accurate quotation must include "the Washington Post's henhouse" (or mark the elision).

Also: "The concern that a filter team may err by neglect, by malice, or by honest difference of opinion is heightened where its institutional interests are so directly at odds with the press freedom values at stake."
