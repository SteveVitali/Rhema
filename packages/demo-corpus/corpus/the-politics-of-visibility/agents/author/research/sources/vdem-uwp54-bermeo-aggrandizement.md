# vdem-uwp54-bermeo-aggrandizement
- **URL:** https://www.v-dem.net/media/publications/UWP_54.pdf · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C21
- **What it establishes:** V-Dem Institute Users Working Paper 54 ("Beyond Democratic Backsliding: Executive Aggrandizement and...") quotes Bermeo's definition of executive aggrandizement verbatim with page citation, providing a checkable source for her in-text wording.
- **Key passages:**
  - Quoting Bermeo: executive aggrandizement is "a form of backsliding [that] occurs when elected executives weaken checks on executive power one by one, undertaking a series of institutional changes that hamper the power of opposition forces to challenge executive preferences" (Bermeo 2016, 10).
  - Paper's gloss: incumbents "use their democratic mandate to evade oversight by other democratic institutions, as well as legal constraints on their power."
- **Reliability:** Secondary (scholarly working paper quoting the primary text with page cite).
- **Relevance to this project:** C21 (the "one by one" / piecemeal, gradual character of executive aggrandizement, quotable at Bermeo 2016, 10).
