# clearinghouse-khalil-v-joyce
- **URL:** https://clearinghouse.net/case/46209/ · **Accessed:** 2026-08-20 · **For:** v1 drafting, claim C24
- **What it establishes:** Full procedural timeline of Khalil v. Joyce/Trump (D.N.J. habeas + Third Circuit). DHS detained Khalil March 8, 2025; government's notice to appear (March 9, 2025) charged removability under INA § 237(a)(4)(C)(i), the rarely-used foreign-policy provision. Judge Michael Farbiarz (D.N.J.): June 11, 2025 preliminary injunction finding the Secretary of State's determination likely unconstitutional; June 20, 2025 bail release (no monitoring, no bond). Sept. 12, 2025: immigration judge ordered removal to Algeria or Syria; district-court stay remained. Jan. 15, 2026: Third Circuit (2-1) vacated the district court orders on jurisdictional grounds (INA channels review away from habeas). May 22, 2026: en banc denied 6-5; June 3, 2026: mandate stayed 90 days pending a cert petition. BIA removal order April 9, 2026; Khalil petition for review filed in the Fifth Circuit May 9, 2026. As of latest entries Khalil remains in the U.S., free on bail conditions (passport surrendered, travel restricted).
- **Key passages:** Third Circuit held the INA "channels judicial review of all questions of law...arising from any action taken or proceeding brought to remove an alien from the United States" away from habeas review. District court found irreparable harm including "missing the birth of his child, damage to his free speech rights, and loss of income and reputation."
