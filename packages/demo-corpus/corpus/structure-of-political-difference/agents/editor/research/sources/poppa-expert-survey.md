# poppa-expert-survey

- **URL:** https://poppa-data.eu/ · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v1 review Phase 2
- **What it establishes:** POPPA (Meijers & Zaslove, founded 2017): expert survey measuring party-level populism and related attributes — core populism characteristics, political style, ideology, organization, democratic commitment, willingness to compromise — for 250 parties/28 countries (2018) and 312 parties/31 countries (2023). Data on Harvard Dataverse. (The site excerpt did not enumerate the sub-dimensions; published POPPA papers measure people-centrism, anti-elitism, Manichean worldview, general-will, indivisibility of the people — not verified on-page this session.)
- **Key passages:** "aims to measure key attributes of political parties in European democracies using expert judgments."
- **Reliability:** Primary project site; sub-dimension detail is from my prior knowledge flagged as unverified this session.
- **Relevance to this project:** Together with CHES's anti-elite items (which the draft does cite via [^7] and [^5]), POPPA shows that disaggregated, multi-facet populism measurement at party level already exists as infrastructure. The draft's System-axis facet profile is best presented as a public-facing wrapper for this existing practice, not a new measurement idea.
