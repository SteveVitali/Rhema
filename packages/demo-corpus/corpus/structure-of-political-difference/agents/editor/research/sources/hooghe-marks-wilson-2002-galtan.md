# hooghe-marks-wilson-2002-galtan

- **URL:** https://garymarks.web.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/13018/2016/09/hooghe-marks-wilson-does-leftright-structure-party-positions-on-European-integration.pdf (PDF read) · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v1 review Phase 2
- **What it establishes:** Hooghe, Marks & Wilson 2002 (*Comparative Political Studies* 35:8) define the "new politics" dimension: **GAL (green/alternative/libertarian) vs TAN (traditionalism/authority/nationalism)**, conceptually distinct from economic left-right. TAN is the strongest predictor of opposition to European integration. This is the two-dimensional framework underlying the Chapel Hill Expert Survey the draft uses for its own empirical exercise.
- **Key passages:** GAL/TAN contrasts green/alternative/libertarian positions with traditionalism/authority/nationalism; the new-politics dimension operates independently of left-right while correlating with it.
- **Reliability:** Primary academic source (author-hosted copy of published article).
- **Relevance to this project:** The draft's finding that Culture + Coercion + Belonging correlate at .78–.82 in CHES 2024 is, almost by construction, a rediscovery of GAL/TAN — the survey was designed around that bundle (TAN = the draft's Tradition+Authority+Communal poles, literally). The draft cites CHES but not Hooghe-Marks, so it presents as an "exactly what the framework should predict" result something a political scientist will read as "the framework split one known dimension into three and found it is one dimension." The draft should cite GAL/TAN and argue explicitly why decomposing it into three questions adds resolution (its Trump/Milei and welfare-nationalist cases actually do this work implicitly).
