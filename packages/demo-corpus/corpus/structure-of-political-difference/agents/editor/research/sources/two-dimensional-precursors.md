# two-dimensional-precursors

- **URL:** Eysenck: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nolan_Chart and https://jonjayray.tripod.com/eysatt.html (search-summary level); Kitschelt: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Kitschelt and https://doi.org/10.1177/00104140261448417 (search-summary level); Inglehart–Norris: https://www.pippanorris.com/cultural-backlash-1 and https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/lsereviewofbooks/2019/06/05/book-review-cultural-backlash-trump-brexit-and-authoritarian-populism-by-pippa-norris-and-ronald-inglehart/ (search-summary level) · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v1 review Phase 2
- **What it establishes:** (a) **Eysenck 1954**: factor analysis yields two dimensions — Radicalism↔Conservatism (R) and tough-↔tender-mindedness (T); Stalinists and Nazis both score tough-minded — the earliest psychometric "second axis." (b) **Kitschelt 1994**: postindustrial competition runs along a left-libertarian ↔ right-authoritarian **diagonal**; voter preferences cluster in two corners of the 2D space (economic redistribution × libertarian/authoritarian values). (c) **Inglehart–Norris, Cultural Backlash (2019)**: authoritarian-populist surge as backlash by holders of materialist/authoritarian values against postmaterialist value change; populism measured on a cultural/authoritarian dimension distinct from economics.
- **Key passages:** Second-hand via search summaries; none of the three primary texts read this session.
- **Reliability:** Search-summary level; all three characterizations are textbook-standard.
- **Relevance to this project:** Completes the academic lineage: the "two dimensions, culturally bundled on a diagonal" finding is 70 years old (Eysenck) and 30 years old in its modern party-competition form (Kitschelt). Kitschelt's diagonal is precisely why the draft's CHES composites correlate: European party systems occupy a diagonal in the space the draft decomposes. The draft's §II hedge is compatible with all of this but cites none of it.
