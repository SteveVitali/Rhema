# 8values-test

- **URL:** https://8values.github.io/ and https://github.com/8values/8values.github.io · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v1 review Phase 2
- **What it establishes:** The 8values quiz (open-source, MIT-licensed, c. 2017) measures four axes whose pole names are nearly identical to four of the draft's five: **Economic: Equality ↔ Markets** (identical wording to the draft's Economy axis), **Civil: Liberty ↔ Authority** (identical to the draft's Coercion axis), **Societal: Tradition ↔ Progress** (identical to the draft's Culture axis), **Diplomatic: Nation ↔ Globe** (close cousin of the draft's Belonging axis, though 8values frames it as foreign policy/patriotism rather than scope of moral obligation). It also has a "Closest Match" feature that maps a four-number score to a named ideology — a crude precursor of the draft's "coordinates then nearest family name" move — which the developers themselves flag as "much less accurate than the values and axes."
- **Key passages:** "The 8values political quiz measures individuals' political values, including Equality, Nation, Liberty, Tradition, Markets, World, Authority, and Progress." Equality pole: "the economy should distribute value evenly among the populace"; Societal: "traditional values and strict adherence to a moral code" vs "social change and rationality."
- **Reliability:** Primary source (the test's own site and repo). High for what the test says; it is a hobbyist artifact, not scholarship.
- **Relevance to this project:** The single most important uncited neighbor. Four of the draft's five axes replicate 8values' four axes, two with verbatim pole names. Rationalist-adjacent readers will notice immediately; the draft must acknowledge and differentiate (its differentiators: the System axis, the Belonging-vs-foreign-policy distinction, the naming architecture, rules of use, no test).
