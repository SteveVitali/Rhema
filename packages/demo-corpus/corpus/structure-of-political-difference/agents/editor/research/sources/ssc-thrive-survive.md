# ssc-thrive-survive

- **URL:** https://slatestarcodex.com/2013/03/04/a-thrivesurvive-theory-of-the-political-spectrum/ · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v1 review Phase 2
- **What it establishes:** Scott Alexander's answer to "why do unrelated positions cluster?": "rightism is what happens when you're optimizing for surviving an unsafe environment, leftism is what happens when you're optimized for thriving in a safe environment" (zombie-apocalypse vs post-scarcity thought experiments). He concedes it "does not quite explain all left vs. right positions" and that "once an issue is associated with one side or the other, the association is self-perpetuating."
- **Key passages:** As quoted.
- **Reliability:** Primary (the essay itself); a blog theory, but among the most-read taxonomy essays in the rationalist-adjacent readership.
- **Relevance to this project:** Marks the other pole of the SSC-adjacent discourse: a *one-real-underlying-dimension* theory, against which the draft's five-question pluralism is positioned (implicitly). Readers from this community hold three stock positions the draft must navigate: (1) thrive/survive-style monism, (2) 8values-style multi-axis testing, (3) Lewis-style eliminativism. The draft engages none by name. Also note Alexander's self-perpetuation point anticipates the draft's "bundles are historically contingent" claim.
