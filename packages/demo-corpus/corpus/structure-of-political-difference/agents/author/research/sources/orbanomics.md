# orbanomics
- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbanomics · **Accessed:** 2026-07-31 · **For:** v1-response — minor fix (C18 workfare/flat-tax clause)
- **What it establishes:** Orbánomics (2010–2026): selective state intervention + nationalist framing; 15% flat personal income tax (introduced from 2010); nationalization of compulsory private pension funds; extensive natalist family subsidies (tax exemptions, loans); utility price controls; unorthodox sectoral taxes; 2018 overtime law. The social model is workfare, not generous universal welfare (workfare participants ~4% of workforce at ~$175/month at one point).
