# welfare-chauvinism
- **URL:** https://www.populismstudies.org/Vocabulary/welfare-chauvinism/ ; citation verified via https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/000169939003300303 · **Accessed:** 2026-07-31 · **For:** v1-response — MI5 ("Welfare Nationalist" renames welfare chauvinism)
- **What it establishes:** "Welfare chauvinism" coined by Jørgen Goul Andersen & Tor Bjørklund, "Structural Changes and New Cleavages: The Progress Parties in Denmark and Norway," *Acta Sociologica* 33(3), 1990, 195–217 (DOI 10.1177/000169939003300303). Their formulation: "the welfare services should be restricted to 'our own.'" Standard meaning: welfare benefits directed primarily to the native in-group, delineated by citizenship/ethnicity — a cross-cutting stance combining left-leaning welfare for natives with restriction toward non-natives.
