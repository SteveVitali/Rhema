# akkerman-mudde-zaslove-2014
- **URL:** https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0010414013512600 · **Accessed:** 2026-07-31 · **For:** v1-response — MI5 (disaggregated populism measurement is existing practice)
- **What it establishes:** Agnes Akkerman, Cas Mudde & Andrej Zaslove, "How Populist Are the People? Measuring Populist Attitudes in Voters," *Comparative Political Studies* 47(9), 2014. Measures populist attitudes as a scale distinct from — and alongside — pluralist attitudes and elitist attitudes, validated on Dutch voters (N=600); populist-scale scores predict preference for populist parties. Demonstrates that decomposing populism into measurable components, separated from pluralism/elitism, is established measurement practice.
- **Key passages:** abstract: "We devise a measurement of these attitudes and explore their validity by way of using a principal component analysis."
