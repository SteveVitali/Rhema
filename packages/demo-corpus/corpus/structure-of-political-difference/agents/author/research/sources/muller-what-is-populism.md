- **What it establishes:** Jan-Werner Müller, *What Is Populism?*, University of Pennsylvania Press, 2016. Criterion of populism: anti-pluralism — populists claim they, and they alone, represent the people; an exclusive moral monopoly of representation ("populist claims are moral and symbolic rather than empirically testable"; populists assert they represent the whole while denying legitimacy to competitors). In power: occupy the state, mass clientelism, repress civil society.
