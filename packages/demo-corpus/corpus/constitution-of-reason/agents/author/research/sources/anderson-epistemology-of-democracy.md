# anderson-epistemology-of-democracy
- **URL:** https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/The-Epistemology-of-Democracy-Anderson/d5bf4c725dd2393a47a538389602e0d9f3616802 (abstract via search; MUSE gated) · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v2→v3; §IV structural predecessor (Major issue 2)
- **What it establishes:** Elizabeth Anderson, "The Epistemology of Democracy," *Episteme* 3:1–2 (2006). Assesses three epistemic models of democracy — the Condorcet Jury Theorem, the Diversity-Trumps-Ability Theorem, and Dewey's experimentalism — and argues Dewey's is superior because it models the epistemic functions of democracy's constitutive features: epistemic diversity of participants, the interaction of voting with discussion, and **feedback mechanisms** (periodic elections, protest) that keep decisions revisable.
