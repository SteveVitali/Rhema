# fei-when-agents-evolve
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2604.27691 · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v2→v3 response; the empirical card for the deflationist rebuttal (Major issue 1)
- **What it establishes:** Fei (KAUST), Guo, Xiao (Fudan), "When Agents Evolve, Institutions Follow" (arXiv:2604.27691, Apr 2026). Seven historical governance systems implemented as executable multi-agent architectures (Qin-Han, Soviet, Mongol pipelines; Tang Three Departments and US Federal gated pipelines; Edo Bakuhan autonomous cluster; Athenian consensus) + single-agent baseline. Within a single model on single-turn tool-use tasks (PinchBench), best-vs-worst institutional gap: 57.1 pp (MiniMax), 58.6 pp (Kimi), 60.5 pp (Gemini). No institution dominates across models/tasks. **Compute was NOT held constant — and that is the point:** "Tang and US Federal consume the most steps and tokens yet do not consistently lead in score"; Edo hits 83.7% with only 3.0 steps / 48K tokens (Pareto-efficient). Gate-loop failure: on Kimi, Tang's Menxia gate rejects up to 15 consecutive times, averaging 26.7 steps and 89K tokens per task for a 21.6% score — maximal token burn, near-worst outcome.
- **Key passages:** "the gap between the best and worst institution is large: 57.1 percentage points for MiniMax…58.6 for Kimi…60.5 for Gemini"; "No single institution dominates across all models and tasks"; gate-loop: "the reject-revise cycle amplifies step count without improving output quality."
- **Caveat to carry honestly:** on complex multi-step tasks (ClaweBench) the institutional gap compresses ~80% (to ~12 pp) — the topology effect is task-dependent, strongest on decomposable tool-use work.
- **Reliability:** primary (arXiv full text read via HTML).
- **Relevance:** The strongest available answer to "gains = tokens": same model, same tasks, ~60-point spread by governance topology, with the *highest*-token institutions sometimes performing worst. Structure doesn't just channel compute; it can waste or leverage it. Cite in v3's deflationist paragraph; do not overclaim (spread is task-dependent).
