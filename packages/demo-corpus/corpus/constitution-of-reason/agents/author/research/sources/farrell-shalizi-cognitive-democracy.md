# farrell-shalizi-cognitive-democracy
- **URL:** https://bactra.org/weblog/917.html · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v2→v3; nearest ancestor of the synthesis (Major issue 2)
- **What it establishes:** Farrell & Shalizi, "Cognitive Democracy" (2012). Markets, hierarchies, and democracies compared *as information-processing institutions*: markets convey scarcity through prices but homogenize contact between modes of life; hierarchies suppress upward information flow (power blocks honest feedback); democracy combines cognitive diversity with equality and direct exposure of viewpoints. Uses Mercier–Sperber exactly as the draft does: "when one is alone or with people who hold similar views, one's arguments will not be critically evaluated… in arguments among people who disagree but have a common interest in the truth, the confirmation bias contributes to an efficient form of division of cognitive labor." Persistent dissenting minorities (Nemeth) improve group problem-solving; network structure findings — too-fast convergence destroys productive diversity.
