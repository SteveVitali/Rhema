# federalist-51-authorship
- **URL:** https://avalon.law.yale.edu/18th_century/fed51.asp · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v2→v3; verify claim-ledger C17 before acting
- **What it establishes:** Avalon text of Federalist No. 51 is headed "HAMILTON OR MADISON"; published Friday, February 8, 1788, New York Packet. "Ambition must be made to counteract ambition. The interest of the man must be connected with the constitutional rights of the place." is verbatim in the text.
- **Reliability:** primary (Avalon Project document).
- **Relevance:** Confirms C17: quotation verbatim and year right; formal authorship disputed, conventional attribution Madison. v3 hedges the reference: "No. 51, conventionally attributed to Madison."
