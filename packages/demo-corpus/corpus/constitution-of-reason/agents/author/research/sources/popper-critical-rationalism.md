# popper-critical-rationalism
- **URL:** https://iep.utm.edu/karl-popper-critical-ratiotionalism/ · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v2→v3; citation repair at "organized corrigibility" (Major issue 2)
- **What it establishes:** IEP, "Karl Popper: Critical Rationalism" (the URL's misspelling is IEP's own slug). Knowledge grows by conjecture and refutation — "scientists gain knowledge not by proofs but by refutations of good conjectures and by replacing them with new and better ones"; fallibilism without justificationism ("all 'assignments' are conjectural"); extension to institutions: "Only open societies preserve reason, that is, criticism, and as a consequence only open societies can be civilized"; piecemeal reform of criticizable institutions.
- **Reliability:** secondary (reference encyclopedia); adequate for a one-line acknowledgment citation.
- **Relevance:** "Organized corrigibility under conditions of incomplete knowledge" is critical rationalism generalized; v3 credits Popper at that formulation (cite *Conjectures and Refutations* / the open-society application) and differentiates: Popper supplies the logic of error-elimination; the essay's subject is the *machinery* that supplies errors with the standing to eliminate anything.
