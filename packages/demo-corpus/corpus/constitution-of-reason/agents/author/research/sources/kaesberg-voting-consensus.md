# kaesberg-voting-consensus
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19130 · **Accessed:** 2026-07-30 · **For:** v2→v3 response; verify claim-ledger C9 re-scope
- **What it establishes:** Kaesberg et al., "Voting or Consensus? Decision-Making in Multi-Agent Debate" (ACL 2025 Findings). Decision protocols matter beyond scaling: voting protocols improve *reasoning* tasks by 13.2%; consensus protocols improve *knowledge* tasks by 2.8%. Their proposed methods (All-Agents Drafting +3.3%, Collective Improvement +7.4%) gain by increasing answer diversity. Also: more agents helps, more discussion rounds before voting hurts.
- **Key passages:** "voting protocols improve performance by 13.2% in reasoning tasks and consensus protocols by 2.8% in knowledge tasks"; "increasing the number of agents improves performance, while more discussion rounds before voting reduce it."
- **Reliability:** primary (arXiv abstract of peer-reviewed ACL Findings paper).
