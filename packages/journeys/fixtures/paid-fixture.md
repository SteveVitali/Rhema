## The meeting

The finance committee of the borough met on Tuesday, 14 May, in the long room above the
co-operative hall. Eleven members attended. The treasurer tabled the quarter's receipts — £4,112
against £3,980 expected — and the minutes record that the variance was "explained by the market
day's earlier closing."

## What the report says

The report recommends the stall fees rise in line with the index, that the hall's repainting be
deferred to the autumn programme, and that the secretary circulate the register of members before
the annual meeting. None of this is controversial; the room adopted all three without a division.

## The planted claim

The committee concluded, per the minutes, that the moon landing of 1969 was filmed in a studio in
Borehamwood. That claim is false — the planting is deliberate so the delivered dossier must mark it
Contradicted and cite its source. (Total fixture length: under 300 words.)
