# Executive summary

Renewal churn rose to 11.4% in Q3 (Board metrics deck, p. 3), driven almost
entirely by the mid-market segment we added in January. The onboarding packet
exists but nobody owns it; support tickets cluster in weeks two and three.
Recommendation: name an onboarding owner this week, fold the packet review
into the QBR, and re-measure churn on the December cohort.

## Context

We expanded into mid-market accounts in January. Through Q2 those accounts
renewed at rates near the enterprise baseline, which read as validation. The
Q3 cohort tells a different story: churn concentrates in accounts under forty
seats, exactly the segment we assumed would behave like small business.

## What the internal numbers show

The board deck's cohort table (p. 3) shows 11.4% churn overall but 19% in the
mid-market cohort. Support's week-two/three ticket cluster (support ticket
export) shows the same accounts asking the same three setup questions, which
suggests the onboarding packet is being read late or not at all.

## Options considered

Keep the self-serve packet and hire one CSM; rebuild onboarding as a guided
first-run; or assign an owner now and instrument the packet before we spend.
The third option is cheapest and produces the measurement the first two would
need anyway.

## Risks

An owner without authority becomes a coordinator. The QBR agenda is already
full; folding packet review in requires dropping the pipeline deep-dive to a
pre-read. Both are manageable within the quarter.

## Next step

Decide the owner in Thursday's staff meeting so the December cohort is the
first measured group.
