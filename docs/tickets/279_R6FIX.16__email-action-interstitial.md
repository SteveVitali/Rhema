# 279_R6FIX.16 — BL-116: signed email-action links consumed on GET (the RH-31 class)

**Status:** open · **Phase:** post-handoff defect insert · **Prereqs:** merged `main` @ `3a840396` · **Size:** S · **Origin:** BL-116 (handoff RH-A1-5, verified at `277_R6LOW.1`): `GET /api/action/:token` and `GET /action/:token` call `consumeEmailAction` on GET — a mail gateway's link-scanner prefetch burns the one-shot token (the recipient's real click lands on the 410 page), and the `hold` arm calls `holdGate` on GET, pausing the gate countdown ("Wait for me") without the user's intent.
