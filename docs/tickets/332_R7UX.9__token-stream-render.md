## Goal

BL-99 part 2: the chat pane renders incoming `chat.token` deltas into the pending turn's
bubble as they arrive — GD-UX-STREAM.R7's 'tokens later' is now. Ordering + correctness
rules: tokens append to the turn identified by `turnId`, coalesce at frame cadence (one
DOM write per rAF, not per event), survive a mid-stream SSE reconnect without doubling or
dropping text (the seq in the event + the completion event are the reconciliation points),
and the final `chat_turn` completion replaces the streamed text with the canonical turn
record (they must agree — a mismatch renders the canonical record, not the stream).
