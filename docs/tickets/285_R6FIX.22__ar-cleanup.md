## Defect (verified at 277_R6LOW.1; BL-122)

The per-stack Artifact Registry repo had no cleanup policy — image storage grew unbounded, and a
later blanket cleanup risked deleting digest-pinned rollback candidates.

## Change (infra/pulumi/compute.ts)

Cleanup policies on `rhema-${stack}`, per the operator ruling of 2026-11-16:

- `cleanupPolicyDryRun: true` — policies evaluate and report but delete nothing until a later
  operator apply flips the flag (one observation cycle first; deleted digests are unrecoverable).
- `keep-tagged` (KEEP, tagState TAGGED) — release/deploy tags are always rollback-reachable.
- `keep-recent-30` (KEEP, mostRecentVersions keepCount 30) — the rollback window, ≈a month of
  daily deploys per image.
- `delete-untagged-30d` (DELETE, tagState UNTAGGED + olderThan 2592000s) — bounds storage on the
  untagged tail only.
