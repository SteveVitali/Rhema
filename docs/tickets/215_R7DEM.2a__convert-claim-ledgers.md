## Goal
Turn a sanitized claim-ledger markdown file into a `claimLedgerSidecar` that the real validator
accepts, with every claim anchored into the draft it reviewed by the declared confidence ladder.
This lays the converter's shared base for 216 and 217: the markdown readers, the quote-fragment
extractor, the anchor ladder, the source-note index, the label side-table and the override
mechanism.

## In scope — deliverables
1. **Shared markdown readers** `src/convert/md/sections.ts` and `md/quotes.ts` per R2 §5 (H3 bodies
   stop at any H1/H2; bold-bullet, table and list readers; quote fragments). (REQ-CONV-1)
2. **Anchor ladder** `src/convert/anchor.ts`: R2 §3 rungs and confidence constants, block-relative
   offsets from `splitMarkdownBlocks` on the **sanitized** draft, References blocks excluded except
   for `Ref [n]` claims, line hints, section-heading fallback; unresolved claim → anchor omitted.
   Emitted anchors round-trip through `resolveAnchor`. (REQ-CONV-11)
3. **Claim-ledger converter** `src/convert/claim-ledger.ts`: the R2 §1 heading grammar at 100%;
   explicit verdict table with qualifiers preserved in `note`; final tier on escalation; `kind` and
   `sourceCheck` omitted; `sources[]`/`url` per R2 §2.1 so REQ-OBJ-18 holds by construction;
   `inventoryCount` from the header; the summary-count check as a diagnostic. An unmapped verdict
   fails. (REQ-CONV-3, REQ-CONV-5)
4. **Source ids side-table** `labels.json`: 0-based `ordinal`, source id (`C23a`, `III16`,
   `VII3-scope`) kept for display. This ticket creates the side-table schema; 216 extends it with
   import-rule field markers. (REQ-CONV-4)
5. **Source-note index** `src/convert/source-note.ts` (`{slug, title, url?, accessed?}`; bodies stay
   raw), used to recover `(reused note: slug)` and `as in <ID>` evidence. (REQ-CONV-5)
6. **Override mechanism** `src/convert/overrides.ts`: the typed `convert-overrides.json` reader,
   each row citing its source line, with per-row consumption tracking; this ticket implements the
   verdict re-score kind; an unconsumed row fails. 216/217 add their kinds. (REQ-CONV-6)
7. **Validation gate**: every emitted claim-ledger sidecar goes through `parseSidecar`; any
   `failed` aborts with no partial output. (REQ-CONV-2, claim-ledger half; 217 generalizes it to
   every sidecar in the pipeline)
8. **Purity and determinism**: pure `(markdown, ctx) → {sidecar, labels, diagnostics}`, no I/O, no
   clock, sorted keys, stable ordinals. (REQ-CONV-1)
9. **Synthetic goldens** for the claim ledger(s) and the anchor-rung histogram under the 214
   layout, plus the R2 §5 assertions (i)–(vi) that apply to claims, the R2 header negative controls
   for verdicts and REQ-OBJ-18, and an `assertShowcaseFixture` pass on the fixture's Tier-1
   Contradicted claim (the S-6 shape REQ-CORPUS-10 runs on GM v2 in 219). (REQ-CONV-12 amended,
   claims part)
