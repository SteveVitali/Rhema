## Goal

BL-95 (R5 H4, P2): the landing page's sample dossier renders dark sans while the in-app
dossier is cream serif — two visual languages for one artifact; and the landing FAQ uses
native `<details>` triangles. Unify: the landing sample dossier renders through the same
dossier visual system as the in-app view (the `sample-dossier` path + `dossier-view` share
the committed type/color tokens — pick the in-app language as canonical per R5's review
note), and the FAQ gets a styled disclosure control consistent with the design system.

## Spec §§ / anchors

- `apps/web/src/sample-dossier.ts(x)` + `sample-dossier.test.ts`,
  `dossier-view.tsx`/`dossier-labels.tsx`, `demo-dossier-snapshot.test.ts`, and the
  landing surface (public pages — `copy.ts`/`landing` sections). The in-app dossier's
  cream-serif treatment is the canonical side (R5 review note).
- Public pages are SSR/no-JS-sensitive — keep the RV-36 no-JS contract: the FAQ disclosure
  must work with JS disabled (`<details>` stays, only its marker is restyled — do not
  replace it with a JS-dependent control).
- The landing page is public marketing surface — no `ui-*` primitives that pull private
  bundles in; check `spa-env`/`protocol-markers` after changes.
