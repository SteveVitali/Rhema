   sign-in methods, active sessions with revoke (the shared-with-mobile session contract —
   revoking kills the token server-side), delete account.

   Balanced on paid, Base on Free — **no raw model names in this section**), default rigor per
   workflow (Standard), per-project overrides winning over account defaults, confirmation

   two non-configurable rows enforced (spend gate; trial-ending/payment-failed — REQ-ACCT-1);
   quiet-hours editing (push + the in-app deferral P1.19 wired).
5. **Integrations** (REQ-ACCT-2): **one card component** — connect / status / items / disconnect —
   hosting P1.15's providers (Substack public archive + export zip — never cookies; Medium; Google
   Drive `drive.file`, export only). Disconnect removes tokens immediately and keeps ingested
   samples unless the user also removes them; every card states what Rhema reads and never does
   ("We never post to your Substack").
6. **Billing:** renders P1.16's surfaces — plan card, credits by bucket with expiries and the
   rollover projection, top-up packs, auto-reload, caps, invoices (Customer Portal handoff).
7. **Data** (Q-12, minimal): *Export everything* → calls P1.18's account-zip job, delivers the
   emailed link with 7-day expiry; *Delete account* → typed confirmation → the **14-day grace**
   (banner + cancel-during-grace) → hard-delete via the worker's `accountDeletionPlan`
   (REQ-ACCT-4 — anonymized ledger rows + the Stripe customer retained per tax law, nothing else);
   the privacy toggles **"Exclude my usage from product analytics"** (the existing REQ-PROD-15
   flag, carried into the new page) and **"Allow Rhema staff to view my projects for support
   (per request, 72 h)"** — both default off; support access is logged and time-limited, and the
   access log is visible to the user (REQ-VIS-11's surface half — the machinery asserts a
   72-h-expiring grant + an `admin_actions`-class audit row per access); the training-posture and
   residual-risk static text verbatim from §14.7 ("Rhema does not train models on your content.
   Model providers process it under their commercial API terms: [list]. During a review the
   Editor searches the web to check your claims; Rhema limits and monitors what those searches
   can carry, but a search query is text that leaves Rhema."); no DPA or SOC 2 badge — one line
   "Enterprise? Ask us about a DPA".
8. **The preference-profile posture copy** (REQ-OBJ-14 — capture-only at MVP): the §11.7 toggle

9. **Project settings** at `/p/:project/settings`: voice link, protocol & venue (the REQ-GENRE-2
