## Goal
A visitor on `/demo` can take an 11-step, non-modal guided tour of *The Structure of Political
Difference* across today's surfaces. Every quoted fragment is verbatim from the committed real
snapshot, pinned at corpus file:line, and every count is derived from it. They can reach the demo
from the landing hero, `/showcase` and the footer, always by document navigation. Demo copy is held
to the funnel's honesty lints. The tour is pinned to current surfaces now and re-pinned by
R7TOUR.2 after R7b.
