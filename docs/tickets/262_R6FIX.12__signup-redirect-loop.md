## Fix

No combination of {stored intent, `?entry=` query} × {0 or ≥1 own projects} × {Showcase unopened/opened/archived} × {magic-link or OAuth landing} may produce a redirect cycle; draft/outline/drop-zone paths land on the working upload/create flow. Preferred shape (per the operator): `GET /app/new` with `entry=draft|outline` (+`held`) serves the SPA shell so the in-router leaf handles it — a GET still never creates. The intent is consumed ONCE — a later `/app` visit must not force-redirect again. Choose the final shape from the SPA router's actual behavior and record the reason.
