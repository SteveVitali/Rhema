## Goal
Every word a stranger reads is product copy, not build notes, and says only what is true of the product they
can use. The live review found (evidence in `docs/build/reports/2026-09-30_prod-design-review.md`):
- **PR-U1.** "Private beta" everywhere on an open-signup product:
  - `COPY.footer` "Rhēma · private beta" (`apps/web/src/copy.ts` ~2123)
  - `/invite` "Rhēma is in private beta." (~11)
  - "Have an invitation? Sign in" (~2055)
  - the invitation notes (~27–30)
- **PR-U2.** Build jargon:
  - "the fixture the Showcase ships" (~1982)
  - the landing Changelog body "Phase 1 — the full IDE, the public funnel, and the Showcase launch together"
    (~2031)
  - "The Assistant lands here in a later pass." while chat is live (~1582)
  - "Tags land in 1.x" (~1370)
  - "Venue bounds as of {dates} — not yet re-verified" (~118)
  - the palette's "… is a 1.x workflow — not yet available." (`packages/commands/src/verbs.ts` `disabledReason`)
- **PR-U3.** Preferences → "Rigor per workflow" lists all 22 `WORKFLOW_KEYS` (`apps/web/src/pages.tsx` ~4455–4470),
  including never-built workflows.
- **PR-U4.** The Inbox prints "Waiting on your call: 0" (`pages.tsx` ~5943–5946) above two "Partial-results
  decision is waiting on you" rows, alongside many "Run recovered" notices and rows with no project name.
- **PR-U8.**
  - Every `<title>` is "Rhēma" (`apps/web/src/render.tsx` ~74 `DOC_TITLE`), with no meta description and no
    OG/Twitter tags.
  - The favicon 404s.
  - `SectionTitle` renders an `<h1>` (`packages/ui/src/shell.tsx` ~71), so pages carry several.
  - `/showcase` 404s (no route in `apps/web/src/routes.tsx`) while the landing names it.
- **PR-U9.** The magic-link email (`apps/api/src/server.ts` ~130–136) is one bare link, "Sign in to Rhema": no
  macron, no expiry, no safety line, and one subject that Gmail threads across sign-ins.

## In scope — deliverables
1. **Mode-true signup copy** (PR-U1 copy half; REQ-BETA-19 note).
   - The footer, `/invite`, the signup/login notes and the sign-in link text render from `signup.mode`
     (`server.ts` ~204 `signupOpen`).
   - Under `open`: no "private beta", no "invitation required", no "Have an invitation?".
   - Under `invite`: today's copy.
   - Field order is SEC.2's; this ticket changes only copy.
2. **The jargon sweep** (PR-U2; REQ-SPEC-2 copy half).
   - Rewrite each string above as user copy. The Changelog section stays (§15 twelve sections) with real
     user-facing notes.
   - The venue line stays **honest**: H4's verification is still pending, so it may not imply verification.
   - Add a test that fails if a listed build term reappears in `COPY`, the verbs' user strings, or rendered
     marketing and settings pages. The terms: "fixture", "Phase 1", "1.x", "later pass", "lands here", and
     "private beta" under open. Extend `no-banned-strings` or `copy.test.ts` (owned decision).
   - The harness is never named "Claude Code" (AGENTS.md).
3. **Unbuilt workflows hidden** (PR-U3). Preferences' per-workflow rigor, and any other settings list of
   workflows, render only launched workflows from **one** source: the `verbs.ts` `phase` registry or an
   equivalent single launched-set (owned decision). The settings API rejects rigor for an unlaunched workflow.
   The palette may keep unbuilt verbs discoverable, but only as "Not available yet", never "1.x".
4. **Honest Inbox** (PR-U4; REQ-IDE-41; REQ-IDE-2 badge rule kept).
   - The heading count equals the rows the page shows as waiting on the user. Either the "partial-results
     decision" rows are real `resume_partial` approvals counted by the badge rule, or their copy stops saying
     "waiting on you" (owned decision, per §5.11).
   - "Run recovered" collapses per run and stops appearing as a user-attention row (§5.11 collapse key = run id).
   - Every row names its project.
   - The empty/zero states follow the §5.9 catalogue.
5. **Page meta and structure** (PR-U8).
   - A per-route `<title>` ("<page> · Rhēma"), a meta description, OG/Twitter tags on public pages, and a
     served favicon (added to the asset parity HOT.1 introduced).
   - Exactly one `<h1>` per page: `SectionTitle` becomes `<h2>`, and the page title is the h1.
   - `/showcase` resolves: signed in → the user's own Showcase project; signed out → the landing's read-only
     sample dossier (owned decision; REQ-GROWTH-11). The Showcase dossier's render is HOT.1's.
6. **Branded sign-in email** (PR-U9).
   - "Rhēma" with the macron, a real button plus a text fallback, and an expiry line **derived from the
     configured magic-link `expiresIn`** (never hard-coded).
   - A "didn't request this? ignore it" safety line.
   - A subject distinct per send that never carries the token (e.g. includes the time requested), so sign-ins
     don't thread.
   - A plain-text part.
   - Tests pin the template and that the token appears only in the link.
7. **Claims consumed, not written** (C1 copy half; REQ-SPEC-32(b) via ACT.1). Any trial, pricing or "what it
   covers" sentence UX.1 touches renders from ACT.1's claim model and EST.1's estimator. UX.1 adds no numeric or
   trial claim of its own; its copy around those claims stays jargon-free.
8. **Stage B.** Deploy, then run the JOURNEY.1 legs:
   - copy under open mode (no "private beta" on any public page)
   - titles and meta per public route, the favicon 200, one h1
   - `/showcase` → the Showcase dossier
   - the Inbox count equals its waiting rows
   - one real magic-link email read from the test mailbox (subject, macron, expiry, safety line)

   Mark the copy half of step **C1** green once ACT.1's claim half is green; otherwise record it gate-pending.
