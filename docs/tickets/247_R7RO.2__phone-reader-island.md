## In scope — deliverables
1. **Island shrink** (REQ-RELOAD-7; UXC-7).
   - `client/ide.ts` keeps only what the phone-UA reader uses: the reader stepper / font preset,
     theme and paper writes, the reader's entry-point disclosures, and the REQ-MOBILE-3 inputs'
     enhancement.
   - Pane toggles and drags, the palette, the full keymap dispatcher, the selection popover and
     anything the SPA controller owns are deleted.
   - Island size before and after is reported.
2. **REQ-MOBILE-3 entry points** (S-5; F4).
   - The phone-UA reader for real projects gains entries to the **timeline**, **findings** and
     **chat**.
   - It carries §10.1's sub-paragraph inputs: gate actions, dispositions with a note, and chat.
   - These are server-rendered, no-JS-capable forms using KIT-1-styled native controls (RV-36),
     reachable from the phonebar.
   - There are no dead-end links: every email or notification deep link resolves to a reader
     surface.
   - Desktop-only subtrees still never enter the DOM (`frontend-spa-spec.md:216`).
3. **Android tablets** (UXC-8).
   - `isPhoneRequest` classifies on `Mobi` and `Sec-CH-UA-Mobile: ?1` only, so `Android` alone is
     no longer a phone.
   - Android tablets get the desktop / SPA path and PANE-2's tablet breakpoints.
   - UA fixture tests cover iPhone, Android phone, Android tablet, iPad (desktop UA) and desktop.
4. **Demo stays SPA on phones** (ADR-126; DACC-4). This is a regression guard: a phone-UA request
   to `/demo/<essay>/…` renders the SPA-compact read-only demo, never the reader.
5. **The note form on the phone reader** (from 238). REQ-MOBILE-3 does not list notes. Decide
   keep or remove for the reader, and record the decision.
