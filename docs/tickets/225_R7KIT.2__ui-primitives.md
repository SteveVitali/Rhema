## Goal
Lay the foundations of round 7's one component kit. Before any dependency lands, the SPA entry
budget is re-measured at the tip, and every later Stream-U ticket reports its entry delta against
that baseline. `@rhema/ui` then gains the whole ADR-129 dependency stack: per-package Radix
primitives, `cmdk` and `lucide-react`, plus a generated `uiCss()`, an entry-graph guard, and the
**shell-critical** primitives that 226's AppShell needs. These are Tooltip, DropdownMenu,
Dialog/Sheet, Button/IconButton, Badge, Kbd, Skeleton and Toast. The form and menu primitives and the
no-raw-controls rule are 225a's. No surface is migrated in this ticket.

## In scope — deliverables
1. **Fresh tip budget, measured first** (REQ-KIT-5). Before any dependency is added:
   - build `@rhema/web` clean at the ticket's base and run `pnpm check:spa-budget`;
   - record entry, total-JS and CSS gzip figures in the run ledger with the SHA;
   - commit them as a baseline, plus a **delta report** in `scripts/check-spa-budget.mjs` that
     every later Stream-U ticket runs to state its entry delta against the ≤ 15 kB gz allowance.

   No `spa-budget.json` ceiling changes.
2. **Dependencies: the whole stack, once** (REQ-DOPS-8).
   - `@radix-ui/react-{dialog,popover,dropdown-menu,context-menu,select,tabs,tooltip,checkbox,radio-group,switch}`;
   - `lucide-react` and `cmdk`.

   Each is declared in `packages/ui/package.json`, pinned to an exact version and checked by
   `check:licenses`. ADR-129 lists every one with its version and licence. No Radix
   meta-package. The packages whose wrappers are 225a's are added here, so the licence and ADR
   listing happen in one place.
3. **Shell-critical primitives** under `packages/ui/src/primitives/` (REQ-KIT-2, shell-critical
   part):
   - **Radix wrappers:** Tooltip, DropdownMenu and Dialog.
   - **In-house:** Button and IconButton (`aria-label` is a required prop), Badge, Kbd, Skeleton,
     a Toast queue, and Sheet (on Radix Dialog, including a **non-modal** variant with no focus
     trap; REQ-IDE-41 is kept).

   Every primitive forwards `ref`, `data-*` and ARIA props, so DOM-contract hooks survive the
   KIT-3 migrations unchanged. Primitives carry no user-facing text (REQ-UX-12). The exports of
   the existing `primitives.tsx` (`Pill`, `Chip`, `FetchedUrlChip`, `ActionLink`) move into the
   directory with their rendered behaviour unchanged. 225a turns `Chip` into the two-variant Chip.
4. **`uiCss()`** in `@rhema/ui` (ADR-129; REQ-KIT-2, REQ-KIT-5):
   - generated from the tokens, with no inline spacing, type, colour or radius literals in
     primitives;
   - inlined beside `themeCss()`/`ideCss()` in the SPA shell and the SSR heads;
   - light and dark through the tokens;
   - an asserted bound of ≤ 24 kB raw per shell document. 225a's additions must fit the same
     bound.
5. **Entry-graph guard** (REQ-KIT-5). A check fails if Dialog, Select, Popover or ContextMenu code
   is reachable from the SPA entry graph. It keys on the `@radix-ui/*` package paths, so it
   already covers 225a's wrappers. The shell's allowance list is exactly Tooltip and
   DropdownMenu. Per-package imports are lint-enforced: no barrel or meta import of
   `@radix-ui/*`, and named `lucide-react` icon imports only.
6. **ADR-129** filled in from R7OPEN.1's shell:
   - the stack;
   - the per-package import policy;
   - the ≤ 15 kB entry-delta rule and how it is reported;
   - the `uiCss()` bound;
   - the native-fallback doctrine (RV-36).

   The SplitPane choice is left open for 229, and 225a appends the GUARD-1 rule mechanism.
