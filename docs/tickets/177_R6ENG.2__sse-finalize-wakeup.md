## Goal
Close the publish/commit race at run end. Today a run's terminal events ride the 10 s safety poll
instead of a fanout wakeup, which pushes event-delivery p95 to 2,238 ms against the ≤ 2,000 ms
target.

## In scope — deliverables
1. A post-commit wakeup on the finalize path, so the terminal batch's publish happens after its
   transaction commits (or an equivalent design that removes the race).
2. A deterministic test reproducing the race: without the fix, the terminal events arrive on the
   safety poll; with it, they arrive on a wakeup.
3. The live re-read recipe for REQ-POST-8, committed in the run ledger.
